# SPEC — clock-gauge realization A/B: pivot pin vs sum-to-zero under a one-sided excursion

**Source analysis:** `docs/REPORT_night305_day129_investigation.md` §Q2 (the
ratchet mechanism), `docs/REPORT_day129_frame_guards_phase01.md` §6 (the
arming pass this follows), and the falsified-approaches archive entry "Clock
sum-to-zero S-basis — RETAINED feature; not the consumer fix" (Jul-12).
Control artifacts: `runs/day129_cont/seg01/` (pin, unguarded, full day),
`runs/q5/arm{A,B}/` (consumer replay of its products), `runs/day129_guards/`
(pin, guarded, 13:00 lineage — the Phase-2 validation chain).

**The question (one paragraph).** Under the default pin realization the
20:47:00 one-sided common-mode excursion ratcheted into the pivot's
near-static CB carry (+49 m, τ = 3.79 h) because the pivot's pinned clock is
the one place the constellation-common direction cannot flex.  Under the
retained alternative realization `network.clock_datum_sum_to_zero`
(per-constellation Σδtˢ = 0 row, exact S-transform of the pin, pin−s2z
constant ~1e-5 m) there IS no pivot: the sat-clock block cannot express a
common jump at all.  The excursion is not gauge — it is a genuine data
inconsistency (σ̂0_code jumped 3.5×) — so the least-squares distribution of
that inconsistency across the remaining states depends on the realization.
Where does it land, and does the WIRE damage (emitted CLK+CB) shrink,
relocate, or persist?  The Jul-12 falsification does NOT answer this: it
falsified s2z as the consumer/AR fix (G29 numerically identical, common mode
consumer-absorbable) and predates the ratchet discovery entirely.

**Hypotheses (pre-registered).**
- **H1 (relocate off-wire):** the observationally-equivalent receiver-common
  direction absorbs the excursion in the 304 soft receiver clocks; the
  residual pressure at the datum receiver's pin lands in MBAR00UGA's LOCAL
  states — which are not emitted.  Wire-level between-sat total-IF shows no
  multi-hour offset on any satellite.
- **H2 (redistribute on-wire):** the pressure re-enters the emitted split
  through the CODE-bias datum (per-(const,signal) sum-to-zero channels +
  per-sat IF pins — which exist under EITHER clock gauge) and smears across
  satellites/channels instead of concentrating on one.  Total damage similar,
  spread differently (possibly worse: every satellite slightly wrong beats
  one satellite very wrong for some consumers, not for others).
- **H0:** no material difference (the inconsistency lands in iono/ZWD/
  ambiguity states identically under both gauges).

**Design consequence to record regardless of outcome:** the Phase-2.1
detector statistic (per-epoch median |Δclock| over satellites) is
REALIZATION-COUPLED.  Under s2z the solved satellite clocks cannot jump
commonly (the constraint row holds Σ = 0 per epoch), so the same physical
excursion produces ~no median |Δclock| signal — the guard would need a
different statistic (receiver-clock common Δ, or the P1-4 σ̂0_pf_code
channel).  This is an argument FOR running this arm unguarded, and a
migration cost if s2z were ever adopted.
