\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

This document is the mathematical companion to \texttt{GnssProcessor}, a
C\texttt{++}17 post-processing and real-time GNSS engine implementing
PPP, PPP-RTK (single-receiver and network), and double-differenced
baseline modes.  Its scope is the estimation mathematics and design
rationale: the material that ages slowly, because it is settled theory
or algebra.  This revision describes the source tree of 30 October 2026
(commit \texttt{5127429}).

Two configuration files fix what the network runs with, and the text
calls them the \emph{reference configurations}:
\texttt{config/golden/golden\_field\_refound.json} (real data, 58
stations) and \texttt{config/golden/golden\_sim\_refound.json} (a
simulated network of 60 stations).  Both use GPS L1/L2 and Galileo
E1/E5a.  A setting described as default off is off in the loader; the
verdict on every network setting is recorded in
\texttt{docs/CONFIG\_promotion\_ledger.json}.

The reference is organised as a set of \emph{component documents}, each of
which also compiles to a standalone PDF.  This combined volume gathers
them in roughly the order the engine applies them:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Foundations} (\Cref{sec:notation,sec:state,sec:obs,sec:diff}):
      notation; the variable-dimension state vector, its declared state
      families and the network's station parameter set; the
      \emph{uncombined} pseudorange / carrier-phase observation model,
      written once and shared by the user filter, the network estimator
      and the state seeds; and the between-satellite single-difference
      (BSSD) and double-difference (DD) transformations.  Every other
      component builds on this.
\item \textbf{Reference systems and measurement models}
      (\Cref{sec:frames,sec:trf,sec:products,sec:atmos,sec:sitedisp,sec:obsphys}):
      the deterministic substrate and physics behind the observation
      model the Foundations document writes abstractly: the coordinate
      frames and time systems every other chapter assumes, together with the terrestrial
      reference frames, datum transformations, and station-coordinate
      epoch propagation that relate them to national frames and published
      coordinates; the baseline
      satellite orbit/clock product evaluation that SSR refines;
      tropospheric and ionospheric delay modelling; the geophysical
      site displacements (solid Earth, ocean, and pole tides) that
      correct the receiver coordinate; and the antenna, attitude,
      relativity, and range corrections that map the raw measurement to
      the modelled observable.
\item \textbf{Estimation engine} (\Cref{sec:kf,sec:stoch,sec:estinfra}):
      the variable-dimension Kalman filter (Joseph-form update,
      prior-anchored iterated relinearisation, robust gating) and the
      backward RTS smoother; the measurement stochastic model and the
      a-posteriori quality-control layer (variance factor, Baarda
      data-snooping, minimal detectable bias, the block test on a line
      of sight, variance components of the carried priors, and the
      network's RAIM tiers); and the supporting infrastructure that
      bootstraps the filter: the weighted least-squares seed, the
      first-epoch residual-difference state initialisation, and the
      pivot selection / disturbance logic.
\item \textbf{Cycle-slip detection and repair} (\Cref{sec:slip}): the
      linear-combination test statistics (Melbourne--W\"ubbena,
      geometry-free, GRAPHIC, extra-wide-lane, ionosphere-free time
      difference), the network's slip layer and its slip funnel, and the
      TurboEdit $2\times2$ integer repair.
\item \textbf{Integer ambiguity resolution} (\Cref{sec:ar}): the
      \textsc{MLAMBDA} integer least-squares search, wide-lane /
      narrow-lane decomposition, validation, constrained feedback and
      fix-and-hold, and the network's integer machinery (the observation
      graph and its bases, the block search, the fixed-cycle fold), all
      default off.
\item \textbf{Inertial aiding} (\Cref{sec:imu}): the strapdown
      mechanisation and 15-state INS error model of the tightly-coupled
      IMU path.
\item \textbf{Real-time SSR corrections} (\Cref{sec:ssr}): how State
      Space Representation orbit, clock, and bias corrections refine the
      undifferenced observation model, and how the user's filter answers
      a step in the network's phase biases.
\item \textbf{Real-time engine} (\Cref{sec:rt}): the streaming
      architecture: NTRIP and TCP transport, RTCM/MSM decoding,
      observation/SSR time synchronisation, the per-epoch real-time
      update path, the pre-update innovation screen, the
      broadcast-baseline jump response and the filter-state snapshot.
\item \textbf{Network mode} (\Cref{sec:net}): the multi-station
      PPP-RTK network estimator that \emph{generates} the SSR-style
      corrections consumed by the single-receiver modes: the
      architecture of the per-epoch loop, the network's parameter set
      and joint observation model, the S-basis datum and its clock,
      phase and satellite code-bias parts, the rank gate, the
      per-station Schur reduction with exact sparse station normals,
      and the constellation scope.
\item \textbf{Between-epoch carry} (\Cref{sec:net-carry}): how
      information reaches that arrowhead system from outside the
      current epoch: the carried families and how each is folded, the
      three folds an arrowhead admits and where each
      must be applied, the double-difference cycle basis (the spanning
      tree's \emph{chords}) that is the only gauge-free quantity worth
      time-linking, the Woodbury algebra that folds it, and a fully
      numerical minimum-parameter example.
\item \textbf{The float ambiguity vehicle} (\Cref{sec:net-vehicle}):
      the carry the reference configurations use: the carried local
      column and its joint local block, the floored birth prior and the
      bounded drop, the held phase-datum basis, and the exact
      conditional time update of the coupling rows with the temporal
      clock anchor.
\item \textbf{Event responses and the emitted product}
      (\Cref{sec:net-wire}): how the network answers slips (one slip
      frees a carried arc once), gap re-entry, re-admission, wind-up
      restarts and gross line-of-sight faults on its carried state, and
      what it emits: the clock with its step test, the phase-bias
      generation hold and its phase-clock mode, the product contract,
      and the user's answer to a held bias's presence edges.
\item \textbf{Pipeline and architecture diagrams} (\Cref{sec:diagrams}):
      the per-mode data-flow figures (user PPP, real time, the network's
      run, epoch, solve stages, emission and harvest) that place every
      chapter above in its loop.
\item \textbf{Generated reference tables} (\Cref{sec:gentables},
      appendix): processing modes, feature toggles, the cycle-slip
      detector registry, and the state-vector key schema, produced
      directly from the source tree by
      \texttt{scripts/gen\_report\_tables.py} rather than maintained by
      hand.
\item \textbf{Linear algebra supplement} (\Cref{sec:linalg}, appendix):
      study material that ties norms, Hermitian matrices, the singular
      value decomposition and the pseudoinverse to computations in the
      engine through thirteen numerical examples, every number printed
      by \texttt{scripts/linalg\_supplement\_examples.py}.
\end{itemize}

Implementation details (function signatures, class responsibilities,
config field reference) live in the headers under \texttt{include/}
and are rendered via Doxygen.  When this document references a
specific algorithmic component, it cites the responsible \emph{symbol}
(a function, class, or JSON config key) together with its source
file (e.g.\ \texttt{KalmanFilter::update} in
\texttt{src/core/filter/KalmanFilter.cpp}, or
\texttt{network.cold\_clock\_seed}).  Line numbers are deliberately
never cited: they rot within weeks, symbol names do not.

The notation follows the IGS conventions adopted by
\textcite{subirana2013gnss} and \textcite{teunissen2017handbook};
see \Cref{sec:notation} for the symbol table this document depends on.
