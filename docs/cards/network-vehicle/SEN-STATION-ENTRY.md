## 2. Design note

### A. The refusal

- **Where.** `src/core/network/solve/SolveStations.cpp`, the catch of the addStation call (the warn "addStation rejected ... dropping it this epoch" at line 263): any exception from `NetworkNormalEquations::addStation` drops the station for the epoch. The test that throws is the positive-definite test of the station's local block, which fails when one local column has no row and no prior.
- **Why the RPB column is free here.** Under `network.phase_datum_eq34` the receiver phase bias of each tracked phase signal is a local column (`NetworkParamKind::RxPhaseBias`, `src/core/network/assembly/ParamRegistry.cpp`, the block at line 232); with `network.phase_datum_eq34_rpb_in_block` its carry rides in the block, but a station that has not yet entered has no carried value, so the column's only information is its own phase rows. PALM's E5a rows exist only on satellites without a clock, which the epoch drops, so the column is declared and has no row.
- **Fix chosen.** A local column with no data row and no prior row in the epoch is left out of the station's block for that epoch (parked): no estimate, no harvest, its carried value (if any) untouched, its gap clock running. The rest of the station enters. The structural reason: such a column is unobservable this epoch by construction, so the epoch has nothing to say about it, and the project's rule is to keep what is usable. A pin or a weak pseudo-row would put an invented value into the solve. Counter per column family (kept stations, parked columns); one event per park episode (the station, the column, the epochs parked).

### B. The return

- **The entry is not a re-coupling.** `networkSolveStationsTrack` (`src/core/network/solve/SolveStations.cpp`, line 337 on) counts a return only when the station was in the solve before (`ever_in_solve`); a station refused from the first epoch enters as a first sighting with no event, against standing invariant 7 (a station absent while the network runs is decoupled; its entry is a re-coupling).
- **The chain kept the station's edges.** `src/core/network/solve/ChainDatum.cpp`: the election builds its tree over every station of the roster with arcs, not over the stations in the solve, so PALM's edges were born at TOW 259200 and carried as kept edges for 25 epochs while PALM never reached the solve; at its entry they were applied as kept pins (`same_edge`, `Kept`, row armed), with no returning flag on PALM.
- **The first harvest is folded as confident.** The wet delay (`src/core/network/carry/ZwdCarry.cpp`, the harvest at line 364 on, the first-fold edge at line 318) and the phase-leveling carries take the entry epoch's posterior as the carried value and fold it from the next epoch with that epoch's variance plus the process noise, a stiff time link (standing invariant 1) born from one epoch.
- **Which of these makes the -23.4 m** is not proven by the read: the entry solve is pulled by stiff rows that are not the station's data, and both the kept pins and the cold-bound declarations act at that epoch. S2 separates them (section 4).
- **Fix chosen.** A station's entry after an absence while the network ran (including an absence from the first epoch) is a re-coupling event with its identity (refused at, returned at, epochs out, the family that failed). At that entry the station is a cold entry: its carried states (wet delay, leveling, receiver clock, receiver code bias, receiver phase bias, slant ionosphere) are dropped and re-born loose from the entry epoch, and the chain treats the station as returning (its edges are re-elected, not kept). While a station is out nothing of it is harvested or folded (already the case: it is not solved); its edges leave the tree.
- **Deferred, with triage.** The slip detectors and the wind-up arcs keep running on the data of a refused station; that is what the uninterrupted run does too and is field-inert for the entry (the arcs are kept, the phase is continuous). Not deferred: everything listed in the fix.

## 2026-11-22: A revised before the first two-hour run (the smoke)

**What the smoke showed.** The 10-epoch smoke of arm a3 with both keys (`runs_nom/_rap_sen_s3_smk`, engine `gnss_processor_sen1.exe`) still refused PALM00ATA at every epoch, and the park counted 0 rowless columns. The null vector of the refused block (`network_events.csv`, `sta_pd_dropped` at TOW 259200) is not one empty column: RPB Galileo L1C 0.63, ISB Galileo -0.32, slant ionosphere of E07, E08 and E12 0.32 each (mass RPB 0.40, IONO 0.50, ISB 0.10). With its E5a satellites clockless, every Galileo line of sight at PALM is single-frequency: the code rows give ISB + I and the phase rows on pinned arcs give RPB - I, so (RPB, -ISB, I) is a direction no row informs. The design note's reading of A (one rowless column) named the symptom of the null-vector census's top column, not the direction.

**A as built.** `network.station_rowless_column_park` is replaced by `network.station_local_null_pin` (no run had used the old key). A null direction v of the block the test judges (A_r^T W A_r plus the pre-PD prior) has A_r v = 0, so it is invisible to every row and to the shared border. On a refusal the station is retried once with one row along each eigenvector whose eigenvalue is below 1e-12 of the largest (the kernel's own count), at its linearization point, of weight 1e-6 of the median of the other eigenvalues (tag `local_null_pin`); no other estimate moves. The columns with a weight of at least 0.05 in a pinned direction are left out of every harvest (a column with a post-PD carry keeps its harvest). A rowless column is the special case v = e_c. A station that still fails is dropped as before. Readings S1 to S5 stand as registered, with the new key in place of the old.

**The thresholds and their reasons.** "Null" is an eigenvalue below 1e-12 of the largest: the kernel's own count of the directions its test rejects (`NetworkNormalEquations::addStation`, the null-direction trace), so the pin acts on exactly the directions the refusal names; with pin rows of 1e10 in a block that bar sits about four orders above the numerical noise of the largest eigenvalue (1e-16 relative). The row's weight is 1e-6 of the median of the other eigenvalues: large enough that the pinned direction's pivot clears the kernel's test (1e-12 of the median pivot) by six orders, small enough that a direction pinned while carrying some information of its own is pulled toward its linearization point by at most that small share. **A direction just above the bar is not pinned:** the station then takes the unchanged path (no retry, no row, no counter); if the kernel admits the block, the direction's values come out with their own large variance and are harvested and carried loose at that variance; if the kernel still refuses it, the station is dropped as before. Unit cases: `StationNullPin.ANullDirectionIsPinnedAndNothingElseMoves` (every other estimate, local and border, equals the problem with the direction removed, to 1e-10; the direction's columns are absent from the harvest) and `StationNullPin.ABlockWithoutANullDirectionTakesTheUnchangedPath` (a full-rank block and a weak direction just above the bar: no row, no parked column, counters zero, the columns kept in the harvest).

**S3's entry reading, moved to the pinned direction** (on the managing session's word, before any S3 run): at 12.5 min, when E10 and E25 receive a clock and the direction becomes observable, read the first determined values of PALM's Galileo receiver phase bias, inter-system offset and the slant ionospheres that were pinned, the chain edges of PALM's Galileo arcs made while the direction was pinned (kept pins or made again), and PALM's wet delay and clock in the five epochs around it against c1. If that first determination disturbs the station, B's rule covers it, and it is built that way.

**The second smoke and the coupling bar** (still before any two-hour run). With the null pin of commit `04e9830` (engine `gnss_processor_sen2.exe`) PALM00ATA was admitted at all 10 epochs (3 pin rows per epoch, 21 parked columns: IONO 14, RPB 3, CBIAS 3, ISB Galileo 1), but every epoch was withheld by the observability gate: "observed 1 (near-)zero eigenvalue(s) ... but the declared S-basis dimension is 2 ... the datum is over-specified". One of the three directions below the bar was not purely local: its residue A_r v was small in the station block but reached the shared border through c = A_s^T W A_r v, and a pin along it passes about |c|^2 / ev to the shared parameters, enough to fix a datum direction of the network. The rule is tightened: a direction below the null bar is pinned only when |c|^2 / ev is at most 1e-14 of the station's shared block (A_s^T W A_s, largest diagonal entry), three orders below the gate's own null bar (1e-11 of the largest shared eigenvalue); a coupled direction is left alone and counted (`sen_null_coupled_skipped`), and if the station still fails it is dropped as before. Unit case `StationNullPin.ADirectionCoupledToTheBorderIsNotPinned`.
