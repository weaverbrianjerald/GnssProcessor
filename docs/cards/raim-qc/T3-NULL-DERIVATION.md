# SPEC — the T3-class null: the statistic is scale-blind, so the defect is in the POOL

QUEUE item 4 (`docs/QUEUE_principled_replacements.md`): *"T3-class test statistics: correct nulls,
not recalibrated constants."*  Written 2026-08-14, fitting on `runs/pareref_on` — the null instrument
regenerated by item 1(d) (`SPEC_pivot_visibility_gauge_defect.md` §5.08).

**Headline.**  The bandaid is a Gaussian 6σ constant; the reflex replacement is a corrected
extreme-value quantile.  **Both are wrong, and for the same reason: the statistic cannot see scale
at all.**  It is a ratio of two functionals of the same pool, so any common rescaling of the
residuals cancels exactly — proven by an A/B that rescales every row by **×1.45** and moves the
statistic's median by **×1.025**.  What it *does* see is the pool's internal heterogeneity, and on a
fixture where every station draws the **same** error model that heterogeneity is a **4.7×**
multiplicative station effect and a **2.75×** signal effect.  Removing the station axis alone, fitted
out-of-sample, cuts the false-alarm rate **4.46 % → 0.97 %** at **zero cost in detection power**
(§13: 20/24 injected slips, byte-identical per-rung, 3.9× fewer flags).  The correct replacement is a
**stratified studentization**, not a new threshold.

✅ **MECHANISM (§7.8): the per-station heterogeneity is per-LOS IONOSPHERE ESTIMATION ERROR**,
truth-graded — corr(log station variance factor, log datum-free iono error rms) = **+0.9258**, error
rms 0.11–2.55 m by station.  The per-arc ambiguity absorbs ~96 % of it; the unabsorbed ~4 % lands in
the phase residual in the iono direction, which is L1-dominant.  Ten other candidates falsified
(§7.7).

⚠ **Field check (§12): the structure reproduces and is stronger — station spread 10.5×,
transferability +0.873 — but the remedy's leverage falls to 1.5×, because the field runs with σ̂₀
clamped at the 1.0 floor on 88–96 % of epochs.  In that regime the denominator is a constant and the
statistic is an unnormalized max, so there is nothing for a stratified pool to fix.  Unclamping comes
first on the field.**

So the shipped statistic is

$$S_c \;=\; \frac{\max_{i\in c} |z_i|}{\max\!\bigl(1,\; 1.4826\cdot \operatorname{med}_{j\in P} |z_j|\bigr)},
\qquad z = |v|\sqrt{w},$$

with $|c| = n_c$ small (3 on GPS triple-frequency uncombined: L1C/L2W/L5Q) and $|P| = N$ large
(≈ 560 rows/epoch on the 24-station fixture).

## 2. The invariance theorem — and why it decides the card

$S_c$ is **invariant under $z \mapsto \lambda z$ for any constant $\lambda$** whenever the floor is
not binding: numerator and denominator are both positively homogeneous of degree 1 in $z$.

⚠ When the floor **does** bind, $\hat\sigma_0 \equiv 1$ and $S_c = \lambda\max_{i\in c}|z_i|$ — fully
scale-*sensitive*.  That is not an escape; it is a second, independent defect: **the test is
non-pivotal**, switching between two different statistics depending on whether the epoch's robust
level happens to sit above 1.  On this arm the floor binds on 38 of 114 epochs (concentrated in
ignition), and those epochs average 1.97 flags against 9.51 on the unpinned ones.  In the settled
window $\hat\sigma_0 \in [1.3, 1.6]$, so the invariance argument applies where it matters — and §5
confirms it empirically end-to-end.

Three consequences, covering most of the "obvious" fixes:

1. **No global variance-model correction can change its false-alarm rate.**  Fixing the a-priori σ,
   the elevation weighting, or the redundancy standardization all rescale the pool nearly uniformly
   and therefore cancel.  §5 measures exactly this.
2. **A recalibrated constant `k` cannot work either**, and not only for the reason the pivot spec
   gave (rising implied-scale curve).  Under the *exact* null the shipped `k = 6` is **already
   stricter than a 10⁻⁶ per-cell test** (§3).  A threshold that is too strict cannot cause a 10⁷×
   excess.
3. **What is left is the pool's internal structure.**  $S_c$ measures a cell's position *relative to
   the median of its pool*.  If the pool is a mixture of sub-populations with different scales, the
   numerator is drawn from the widest component while the denominator tracks the most numerous one,
   and the ratio has no fixed null at all — its distribution depends on mixture weights that change
   every epoch as the station/satellite set changes.

On `runs/pareref_on`: T3 = 798/21788 = 3.662567e-02 against `gaussAlpha(6)` = 1.973175e-09 ⇒
**1.86e7×**.  The ladder across the arms on disk, for provenance:

Halving the rate took two datum fixes; the remaining 1.86e7× is what this card is about.  The two
"obvious" corrections are both negligible:

- **Max-over-rows.**  The exact per-cell null is $\alpha_{\text{cell}} = 1-(2\Phi(k)-1)^{n_c} =
  5.9195\text{e-}9$ at $k=6,\,n_c=3$ — **3.00×** the quoted per-row α.  Against 1.86e7× this is a
  rounding error.
- **Denominator noise.**  Simulating $\hat\sigma$ honestly (draw the pool, take its actual median; a
  Gaussian surrogate puts mass near zero and yields absurd quantiles) and integrating the numerator
  in closed form via $P(S>k) = \mathbb{E}_{\hat\sigma}\bigl[1-(2\Phi(k\hat\sigma)-1)^{n_c}\bigr]$:
  the threshold for α = 1e-6 is **5.10** with $\hat\sigma\equiv1$, **5.06** at $N=1400$, **5.00** at
  $N=30$.  The `max(1,·)` floor truncates $\hat\sigma$ from below, so under a nominal null the
  shipped test is *conservative*.

⚠ **The "excess kurtosis 4391" figure does not reproduce on this arm.**  Settled `s_raw` has excess
kurtosis **10.38** (skew 2.70).  4391 belongs to a different quantity or a different arm; do not
carry it forward without re-deriving it.

## 4. Measured: the pool is a mixture, where every station draws the SAME model

`config/sim/simnet24_inject.json` specifies a single global
`noise: {code_sigma_zen_m: 0.3, phase_sigma_zen_m: 0.003}` and **no `multipath` block**, so the
generator defaults apply network-wide (Gauss-Markov phase multipath, 2 mm zenith, τ = 300 s, 1/sin el
amplified to a 20° cap — `include/sim/SimConfig.hpp:150-169`).  Verified against the emitted
per-station child configs (`runs/simnet24_inject_null/_child_*.json`): each carries only its own
`station.seed_salt`, derived from the station name.  So **every station draws an independent
realization of the SAME error model, and there is no per-station σ anywhere in the generator.**
Independent realizations of a common model give a few percent of sampling spread in a robust scale,
not 4.7×.  A systematic per-station scale of that size is manufactured downstream of the data.

Settled window (> t₀+1800 s, `s_raw > 0`): 9975 cells over 53 epochs, 24 stations.

| axis | spread | source |
|---|---|---|
| **station** | **4.7×** (0.948 … 4.452) | fitted cell scale, `network_station_residuals.csv` |
| **signal** | **2.75×** (L2W 0.9974, L5Q 1.3325, **L1C 2.7435**) | `network_pf_keyed.csv` SIG rows, median `s0_phase`, settled window (over all 114 traced epochs: 0.9155 / 1.1656 / 2.3597 = 2.58×) |
| epoch | 1.3× (0.869 … 1.152) | fitted; i.e. the axis σ̂₀ *does* estimate is nearly irrelevant |

The signal axis sits **inside** the cell max — the cell is a max *across* L1C/L2W/L5Q, so it is
essentially always an L1C row, while the pooled median is dominated by L2W/L5Q.  This also rules out
elevation weighting: all three signals share an elevation and a weight at a given (station,
satellite, epoch).

Corroborating structure:

- Flags concentrate in **11 of 24 stations** and **40 (station,satellite) keys**; S20 carries 33.7 %
  of all 798 flags, S06 28.4 %, S01 16.7 %, S07 12.3 %.  Per-station flag rates run to **26.1 %**
  while 13 stations flag at **0.00 %**.
- Flags-per-epoch is **4.13× overdispersed** against Binomial(n_cells, p̂), and the cells are
  **strongly dependent in time**: within-arc lag-1 autocorrelation of `s_norm` is **0.8994** (21 555
  consecutive pairs), and P(flag at t+1 | flag at t, cell still present) = 653/789 = **0.8276**
  against a marginal 0.0366 — a factor **22.6**.  The 798 flags are not 798 events; they are **145
  contiguous arc-runs on 40 (station,satellite) keys**.  This is the measurement R3 turns on: the
  family is nowhere near 21 788 independent tests.
- **No observational covariate explains it**: corr(log station scale, ·) = −0.20 (median elevation),
  −0.03 (fraction below 20°), +0.004 (median truth I_L1), +0.24 (max truth I_L1), from each
  station's `truth_atmo.csv`.
- The residual scale is **non-stationary through ignition** — epoch-median `s_raw` 0.836 (first
  third) → 1.816 (last third).  Grade on a settled window or you are measuring convergence.
- **364 cells (1.67 %) have `s_raw` exactly 0** — a carried ambiguity with no phase row this epoch.
  They can never flag, yet they are counted as T3 opportunities, which deflates the reported rate.

## 5. Falsified: the redundancy standardization (measured, not argued)

**measurement** σ, omitting the redundancy factor ($\operatorname{Var}(v_i) = \sigma^2 r_i/w_i$, so
$z\sim N(0,r_i)$, not $N(0,1)$).  That is *true* and it is *not the cause*.

## 6. The principled replacement

**R1 — studentize per stratum, with a slow/fast split that the noise budget forces.**

$$\hat\sigma(s,t) \;=\; \hat\sigma_{\text{sta}}(s)\cdot\hat\sigma_{\text{net}}(t)$$

- $\hat\sigma_{\text{net}}(t)$ — the existing network-wide robust scale.  **Keep it.**  It is the
  fast channel, and epoch-common contamination is exactly what RAIM exists to catch.
- $\hat\sigma_{\text{sta}}(s)$ — a **slow**, robust, time-pooled per-station factor (EWMA of the
  station's robust residual level, normalized to network median 1), with a floor/ceiling and a
  minimum sample.

⚠ **SUPERSEDED IN FORM by §7.5** — the per-stratum factor should be the variance component
Σw·v²/Σr_i, not a median of cell maxima.  The slow/fast split and the noise budget below still stand;
only the estimator inside the slow factor changes.

The split is arithmetic, not style.  Relative SD of a robust scale from *m* rows,
$1.4826\sqrt{1/(4mf(q)^2)}$ with $f(q)=2\varphi(0.6745)=0.63556$:

| m | SD(σ̂)/σ | what |
|---|---|---|
| 564 | 4.91 % | network pool, one epoch |
| **23** | **24.32 %** | one station, one epoch — **too noisy to studentize against** |
| 415 | 5.73 % | one station, whole window |

A per-station-per-epoch scale would re-import a heavy tail from the *estimator*.  Pooling over time
is what makes it usable, and §4.1's +0.847 half-to-half correlation is what licenses the pooling.

**R2 — declare the stratum, do not absorb it.**  A per-station denominator makes the *cell* test
correct, but on a homoscedastic fixture the heterogeneity it divides out is model error, not noise.
Absorbing it silently would mask a real defect — the same failure mode as recalibrating `k` until the
alarm stops.  So the per-station factor becomes a **first-class declared statistic** with its own
event, counter and threshold: a station whose slow factor departs from the network is precisely the
"bad station" that T4's median-polish row effect was built for and that is currently deliberately
disabled (`NetworkRaim.cpp:434-439`, where `bad_stations` is declared and never populated at `:441`).
This is standing invariant #7 applied to a test statistic — the edge where a station gets its own
scale is an event at both sides, and both must be counted.

**R3 — set α by FDR over the family, not `k` by hand.**  With the pool stratified, the exact per-cell
null is usable directly (`exact_k(α, n_c)`).  But a fixed `k` has an implied family-wise α that
drifts with roster size and run length — 21 788 cells here, ~10× that at 305 stations.
Benjamini–Hochberg over the (cell × epoch) family is the right control.  ⚠ **BH's independence/PRDS
assumption is measurably violated here, and not marginally**: §4's within-arc lag-1 autocorrelation
is 0.8994 and P(flag | flag at t−1) = 0.83 against a 0.037 marginal, so the 21 788 "tests" are closer
to **145 arc-runs on 40 keys**.  Any FDR scheme must be applied to a family whose effective size is
set by the arc structure, not the cell count — and cells additionally share the σ̂₀ denominator and
the shared satellite states.  Benjamini–Yekutieli's $\sum_{i=1}^{M}1/i$ factor is the conservative
fallback; its cost should be measured rather than assumed.

**R4 — population hygiene (cheap, independent).**  Exclude the structurally untestable cells (`s_raw`
exactly 0, 1.67 %) from the T3 opportunity count, and give T3c an opportunity definition that matches
its conditional construction (§1) or drop its `implied_alpha` column, which currently compares a
conditional rate against an unconditional α.

**R5 — do NOT do.**  Do not arm `raim_redundancy_normalize` for this purpose **on this posture**:
measured null on the flag rate, +38 % runtime (§5).  ⚠ **Sim-scoped** — §12.2 shows the field runs
with σ̂₀ clamped at the floor on 88–96 % of epochs, where B2's ×1.45 lift would *unpin* the pool
rather than cancel.  A field B2 arm is owed before this line is read as general.  It may still be
right for MDB, which needs `r_i` for a different reason and hard-errors without it
(`GnssConfigLoadNetwork.cpp:3540-3551`).  Do not recalibrate `k`: under the exact null `k=6` is
already an α = 5.9e-9 per-cell test (§3), and on the settled window a constant would have to be
**k ≥ 14.6 to reach even α = 1e-3, and k ≥ 19.8 for 1e-4** — the latter above all but one cell in the
whole run (max `s_norm` = 19.726).  The test would be dead.

⚠ **And the fixture cannot calibrate a 6σ-class constant empirically anyway.**  9975 settled cells
resolve no rate below ≈ 1e-4; a 5.9e-9 threshold would need ~10⁸ cells, i.e. ~10⁴ of these runs.
That is the standing argument for a *derived* null over a fitted quantile — item 4's premise, and it
survives.
