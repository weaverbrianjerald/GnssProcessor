## 3. 2026-10-11: AGT-4, the chain datum on the shared module

**What landed.** `networkElectChainDatum` (`filter/NetworkChainDatum.cpp`, 223 lines out, 181 in) takes its graph, its components and its spanning forest from `filter/NetworkArcGraph`. Per (constellation, signal) group:

- **The graph.** `buildArcGraph` with the stations in ascending roster index, the satellites in the group's string order and one arc per usable key in `usable_keys` order (the order the components are united in). Each arc carries what the walk reads: its slip bit (`arcSlipped`, so zero unless the slip-aware election is armed), its longevity score (`arcLife`, read only under the longevity election) and the hygiene's host verdict (`eligibleHost`). A satellite name without a canonical `SatId` would be left out of the graph with a warning; no run here printed one.
- **The seed.** `seedEdge` keeps its live filter, its slip filter and `eq34_slip_deseeded`, and now collects the graph's arcs in seed order (`anchor_incumbent`, then `eq34_rx_parent`).
- **The components.** `arcComponents` in the order of `legacyChainVertexRank`: every root is a satellite under the shared link rule, so the components come in the string order of their root satellites, as the string union-find iterated them.
- **Per component, kept:** the r1 election (it reads the component's station indices and satellite names), the RPB pin and the gauge bookkeeping, the bridged satellites (now the walk's further roots), `recordEdge` (its body unchanged; it takes a tree edge and records the child and the parent as graph vertices) and the shift walk (its two maps become vectors over the graph's vertices).
- **Per component, replaced:** the vertex strings, the string union-find, the `stoul` parsing and the two-queue, two-pass loop, by `growChainForest` (roots r1 then the bridged satellites; the incumbent arcs; `refuse_ineligible_first` when the hygiene is active with `min_arc_ep` above 0; `refuse_slipped_first` under the slip-aware election; `by_durability` under the longevity election; the coverage as the station rank; `legacyChainVertexRank` as the second pass's frontier order). After the walk `recordEdge` is replayed over the forest's edges in attachment order, and `hyg.cold_fallback_pins` counts the new edges flagged `ineligible_host`.
- **Unchanged after the group loop:** the rank cross-check, the D4 erase, the carry S-transformation, the I2 merge and the incumbent swap.

- **The knobs.** `cycle_basis` requires `tree_basis`, `decorrelated_partial` and `block_search`; without any of them the loader names the missing one and forces it off. `cycle_basis_sticky` (default true) is the control: false rebuilds the forest and the basis from scratch at every search. Armed, the loader prints its own line and the producer AR summary line gains a basis clause; unarmed, both lines are unchanged.
- **`filter/NetworkArcLife`, the life stamp.** `ArcLifeStamp` holds the seven fields of card AGT section 2, read in place from the arc ledger's admitted side, the leveling carry and `amb_flag_ep` (`arcLifeStamp`, `arcLifeStampOf`); `arcLifeChange` is the bit mask of the changed fields. The admitted run start is the birth epoch, or the epoch after a re-entry (the ledger is Reentered at the return epoch and Tracking one epoch later, and both read the same start), and -1 while the ledger does not hold the arc live. Section 3's binding rule is met by its first option: `carryLifeOf` is the one reader of the carry's life fields, and the suspect mask's per-epoch fold now reads them through it (`filter/NetworkArSuspect.cpp`, decision-identical).
- **The search's track of a life** (`arcLifeStep`). An arc the last search did not meet, an arc in this epoch's free-epoch latch, or an arc whose stamp changed begins a new life; its durability restarts and, when the last search met it, it is slipped. The roster audit and the leveling harvest act after SB_NETAR, so a life a search begins shows up once more at the next search (the ledger admitting the run, the harvest stashing the carry). That change is read as the event's echo and re-baselines the stamp, provided it is confined to the ledger fields, `birth_ep`, `relevel_s` and a carry that appears; an erase, a drop or a flag is never an echo. So each ledger birth or re-entry and each carry erase, drop, re-level or flag gives exactly one life edge (unit suite NetworkArcLife).
- **`filter/NetworkArCycleBasis`, the basis.** Per constellation the two signals with the most arcs form one pair family and each further signal a one-band family (`arCycleFamilies`). The rows carry a present and a slipped bit per band and the weaker band's durability (epochs since its life began). Per component of the arcs present in every band, `growBaselineForest` runs with two common satellites then one, slipped arcs refused in the first pass, the most durable reference, and the last search's links; the component forests are joined and closed by `shortCycleBasis(Full)`. `arCycleMapStates` reads `basisChangeMap` against the stored basis, the slipped band-arcs invalidated, as same, mapped, fresh through an invalidated arc, or fresh outside the stored span. The family memory (`ArBasisMemory`: links, basis, arc tracks, DD first epochs) is `ctx.carry.ar_basis`, stored after the search and not snapshotted; the run tallies are `ctx.counters.netar_cycle`.
- **The seam** (`filter/NetworkProducerAr.cpp`). Each PartnerDd and BorrowedDd cycle becomes one search row (a fundamental fallback is counted, never searched). The readback and the search take one block per station (`netar::searchBlockKey`: `cons|sta_r`, else `cons|sta_r|ref_sta` as before). The trace gains `cycle_kind`, `band_only`, `basis_age_ep` and `map_state`, the epoch's log line a cycle-basis clause, and the run end a three-line clause (coverage per band, links and re-seats, the map, the lives, the cost) with the invariant-4 warnings. The per-signal basis (the tree and the star) moved unchanged into a file-local `arSignalBasisRows`, so the seam reads as pool, basis, covariance, search, trace.
- **Scripts.** `scripts/simnet_producer_ar_grade.py` splits the kept, correct and wrong fixes by `cycle_kind` (with `/band_only`); `scripts/simnet_ar_basis_churn.py` reads the new columns, splits its churn by kind, counts the map states, lists the fresh rows of one epoch (`--fresh-at`) and adds the two churn figures over the DDs whose four arcs survive into the next epoch. Both self-tests pass.

**Two stickiness additions to the module, found on rung 3c and kept.** The design's basis as first built (`runs/_rap_agt5v1_r3c`) kept 98.84 % of the links but only 99.49 % of the DD identities at p50 (minimum 67.3 %), and at TOW 265350 128 of 391 DDs changed at once. The cause was in the incumbent rule: when a link fails mid-tree, Prim picks the outside station sharing the most satellites, which is often a former child of the failed station, so the subtree reverses under the re-seat (S39000SIM's link to S31000SIM became S31000SIM's link to S39000SIM; 29 parent-gone re-seats over the run).

1. **A waiting link waits for its parent** (`growBaselineForest`). A station whose incumbent link still waits for its parent is picked by Prim only when no other outside station reaches the round's threshold, so the re-seated station rejoins first and its children keep their links. Without an incumbent no station waits, so the tree_basis Prim is unchanged. One AGT-1 unit expectation encoded the reversal and is updated (`NetworkArcGraph.BaselineForestReseatsIncumbentLinksByCause`: station 2 now keeps its link to its re-seated parent, parent-gone 2 becomes 1). On rung 3c (`runs/_rap_agt5v2_r3c`): parent-gone re-seats 29 to 0, identity minimum 67.3 to 85.5 %, fixed DDs in the next basis minimum 0 to 87.6 %.
2. **A borrowed closure stays while it still closes its chord** (`shortCycleBasis`, a new optional `sticky` basis). Borrowed DDs (73 of 395 per band) then carried half of the remaining churn (588 of 1 161 lost DDs), because the earlier station sharing the most satellites flips whenever a common-satellite count does. With the last basis passed in, a chord keeps its borrowed partner and reference while the partner is earlier in the closing order and every term is usable; otherwise the rule is unchanged. Rung 3c (`runs/_rap_agt5_r3c`): identity p50 99.75 %, minimum 88.0 %; fixed DDs in the next basis p50 100 %, minimum 89.9 %.

## 5. 2026-10-11: AGT-6, the lattice observatory

**What landed** (all behind `network.ambiguity_resolution.trace_lattice`, default off; measure-only, so not fingerprinted).

- **The knob.** `trace_lattice` requires `cycle_basis`; without it the loader says so and forces it off (so it also goes off with a cycle basis the loader forced off). Armed, the loader prints its own line and the producer AR summary line gains "Lattice observatory ON."; unarmed, both lines are unchanged.
- **What the search hands over** (`ArEpochFixes`, `filter/NetworkArCycleBasis.hpp`). `networkProducerAr` now returns, under the knob, whether the cycle basis was built, the single-signal DDs the search determined (the trace's kept rows), each as its basis cycle with its integer and typed identity (`ArFixedCycle`; the DD row carries its cycle and identity), and the life reading of every band-arc the search read (`ArArcReading`: the `arcLifeStep` edge, the changed stamp fields, and the free-epoch latch's cause bits from `relevel.free_cause`). Unarmed the return is empty and nothing is read.
- **The observatory** (`filter/NetworkArLattice.{hpp,cpp}`). One search epoch is `arLatticeStep`: first `arLatticeReleases` lists every arc a lattice row holds that the search did not read (vanished) or read as a new life (first seen, freed, stamp change), in ascending `ArcId` order, and each is eliminated (`latticeEliminateArc`) with its cause, stamp fields and free causes; then each fix is offered in basis order (`latticeOffer`: added, confirmed, contradicted or not primitive; a contradiction records the implied integer, an addition the new row's length). The seam `networkArLattice` runs it on `ctx.carry.ar_lattice`, adds to `ctx.counters.netar_lattice`, writes the two files and warns on an epoch with a contradiction; it is called inside the SB_NETAR bracket right after the search (`src/core/app/ApplicationNetwork.cpp`: the search's return is kept and the seam called, plus its include). A run-end report (`networkArLatticeRunEnd`) is one new row of `kNetworkRunEndReports` after the suspect-mask row (110 rows to 111). Nothing the solve reads is written.
- **Two files.** `logs/network_ar_lattice.csv` (`kArLatticeHeader`), one row per search epoch: rows, arcs, fixes, the four verdicts, the releases by cause, the releases that held rows, the dimensions lost, the eliminations with no unit row, and the longest and mean row. `logs/network_ar_lattice_events.csv` (`kArLatticeEventsHeader`), one row per release (the arc, its cause, the rows that held it and the rows dropped, the stamp field bits and the free cause bits) and one per offer that is not a confirmation (the DD, the offered integer, the implied one on a contradiction, the row length on an addition); a kept DD of the search trace with no row there was confirmed.
- **The grader** (`scripts/simnet_ar_lattice_grade.py`, self-test PASS). It checks the bookkeeping (the verdicts sum to the fixes, which equal the trace's kept rows; the row count follows added minus dimensions lost; the dimensions lost equal the releases that held rows, epoch by epoch), grades every kept DD's verdict against truth (wind-up branch counted, as `simnet_producer_ar_grade.py` does): a wrong integer confirmed is the lock-in a hold would create, a wrong one added a wrong row, and each contradiction is attributed to the offered integer (a wrong fix) or the implied one (a stale lattice: a missed release or an earlier wrong row). It lists the releases by cause, stamp field and free cause, the arcs released again one epoch after a release, and with `--slip-tow` and `--slip-arc` the releases at the slip epoch.
- **Unit tests** (`tests/test_network_ar_lattice.cpp`): suite NetworkArLattice (7) covers the order (a DD fixed again on an arc freed this epoch is added; offered before the release it would contradict), the attribution (vanished, first seen, freed with its free causes, stamp change with its fields, in `ArcId` order; an echo and an unchanged arc are kept), the dimensions (ten held DDs on a released reference lose one dimension, the reduced form holding the reference in one row; a satellite that sets at four stations costs three, its last arc holding no row), a contradiction (the lattice unchanged, the implied integer reported) and the tallies; suite NetworkArLatticeConfig (4) the default, the interlock (also through a cycle basis forced off) and the two headers.

**Two measured findings for the hold (card ARL task 2).**

1. **A contradiction count of zero is not evidence of a clean release.** On the S8 twin without the slip fixes the stiff carry keeps the float near the pre-slip integer, the search fixes that integer at its usual success rate, and the lattice confirms it every epoch: a missed release produces agreement, not contradiction. A hold's protection has to come from the release seam (the detectors); the lattice's consistency cannot stand in for it.
2. **One float-vehicle drop is read as two lives one epoch apart.** A drop on a stale gap at SB_PREASM and the harvest's gap-prune erase of the same carry land on either side of SB_NETAR, so the search reads a stamp change at the drop epoch and, because an erase is never an echo, another at the next. The search fixes the arc's DDs in between, and the second release drops that row again: 9 such re-releases on rung 3c, 17 at 300 stations, none with the suspect mask armed (it keeps the dropped corner out of the search). Each costs a dimension the next fix restores; no integer was wrong.
