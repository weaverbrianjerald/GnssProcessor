**(2) Why it stops at 37 minutes.** No state reaches a floor then. Only the wet delay reaches its floor (5.0 mm, from 25 minutes), and its term carries no share. The update of a quantity converging as sigma / sqrt(n) is about sigma / n per epoch, so both parts of the move shrink as 1 / n: at 15 to 40 minutes the shared move's median is 18.5 mm on L1C and the own part's 12.6 mm, against a gate of 47.6 mm (L1C) and 61.1 mm (L2W), and the last crossings, 23 at 30 to 35 min and 4 at 35 to 40 min, are the tails of that decay.

**The cause, named.** The stream's phase biases carry two quantities that only the code determines and that converge as the code averages: the network's phase level, realized at one receiver whose phase bias is pinned and whose clock and slant ionosphere are free every epoch, and each satellite's split between its clock and its phase biases. Their moves are invisible to a user's phase in single differences (the level cancels between satellites; the split moves the clock with the phase bias), and the emitter's quarter-cycle value test on the phase bias alone declares them as steps.

#### 13.5 2026-11-14: results of 13.4 (the receiver clock's carry), and part 3 for the cause found

**What ran.** `_rap_irdu_q1rc_nr3` and `_rap_irdu_zwrc_nr3` on `gnss_processor_irdu_fp2.exe`, both exit 0, 240 of 240 epochs. **Reads.**
- **Declared steps in the first 40 minutes:**

  - The plain chain arm: 1 796 (542.02 m against 541.93 m), the last at 39.5 min.

- **The stream bars:** the control's in every bin but one (narrow lane at 60 to 75 min, 0.016 against 0.015 cycle).
- **The users:** every bin equals the control's to the millimetre at every start, with the recipe and with the rule; their resets stay at 1 611, 408 and 27.

**Verdict: FAIL** on all three bars: the shared move is not halved, the steps are not down by a third, and the last step is not earlier. The clock's freedom is not the cause.

Why the carry adds nothing: the root's phase observations, with its phase bias pinned, tie its clock to the network's phase level at every epoch. A clock free at each epoch therefore loses no information about the level. That information comes only from the root's code, whatever model the clock has. A time link whose prediction is uncertain to decimetres (the simulator's clocks wander 0.26 m per epoch at the median) adds nothing at the size of the level's per-epoch updates (centimetres). The root's clock, ionosphere and wet-delay terms of 13.3 are therefore the root's phase fit absorbing the level's motion, an identity of the equations, not a separate driver. The same reasoning applies to a carry of the root's slant ionosphere (its phase ties it to the level as well); that carry is not measured.

**The cause, restated.**
- The stream's phase biases carry two quantities that only the code determines.
  - The network's phase level: every receiver's and every satellite's phase bias against the one receiver whose phase bias is pinned.
  - Each satellite's split between its clock and its phase biases.
- Both converge as the code averages:
  - the formal sigma of the root's clock, which carries the level, follows 0.89 m / sqrt(n);
  - the satellites' clocks follow 0.77 m / sqrt(n).
- Each epoch's update therefore moves every phase bias by roughly that sigma divided by n.
- A user's phase sees neither motion:
  - the level cancels between satellites, for a user whose satellites the stream all corrects (12.9);
  - the split moves the clock together with the phase bias.
- The emitter's quarter-cycle value test on the phase bias alone declares both motions as steps until about 37 minutes.

A check not passed: r3c's root sees 9 satellites against Q1's root's 7, and its clock's formal sigma at 30 min is 0.069 m against 0.114 m. Yet r3c's shared move at 15 to 40 min is only 12 % smaller (16.3 against 18.5 mm on L1C), so the size of the level's motion is not shown to follow its sigma from one bed to the other.

**Part 3: approaches for the cause found** (not built; the author's choice):

1. **Declare on what a user sees.** The emitter tests each satellite's move of its phase-visible correction (the clock less the phase bias, per signal), less the median of that move over the constellation's satellites. Both the level and the split cancel there; a real phase step of one satellite (a re-seat, a slip response) does not.
   - Needs: a value test in `NetworkProductEmitter` and the same rule in the replay consumer, through a token of the S contract so both declare alike; a default-off key; a ledger row.
   - Risks: a step common to every satellite is no longer declared. A user in single differences does not need it; a user that mixes in uncorrected satellites does (12.9, the 12.11 rule). It must be graded on r3c, where re-seats make real steps. Its code-bias analogue needs the same review.
   - Predicted size: 13.2's arm (iii) is an upper bound of the gain on this bed.
2. **The emission hold in its phase-clock mode** (the reference configuration's posture). It removes the level from the emitted phase biases and writes each satellite's own clock step into them.
   - Effect on this bed (the arm with the holds): the declared steps end at 19 minutes; all 542 left are own steps.
   - Needs: three keys, and the precise a priori clock (its posture limit; on the simulator that clock is the truth's).
   - Risk: it changes what the chain's stream carries, which the integer work reads holds-off.
3. **Realize the phase level over every receiver,** a minimum condition on all the receivers' phase biases in place of one receiver's pin. The level would then converge with every receiver's code.
   - Needs: a change of the chain's datum in the estimator.
   - Risks: the receivers' phase biases carry the tree's integers (metres), so the condition is not integer-neutral; the chain's frame and the users' integers would need re-examination. Large.
4. **No value declarations while the network converges** (forced bumps kept), until the level's formal sigma passes a bar (about 40 minutes on this bed).
   - Needs: a gate in the emitter on a sigma it does not hold today.
   - Risk: a real step in that window is not declared.
5. **A value test scaled to the expected update:** declare a move only beyond k times the key's expected convergence update, from its formal sigma.
   - Needs: the per-key formal sigma at the emitter (the phase-bias sigma emission exists, off).
   - Risk: a real step smaller than the expected update hides during convergence.
