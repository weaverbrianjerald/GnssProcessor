## 1. The question

Card ARL's search reads a basis of double differences built by its own greedy tree (section 9 of that card, Prim's algorithm since section 11). The tree covers only the chords whose satellite the link parent tracks, against one reference satellite per link, rebuilt from scratch every epoch, separately per signal. Three consequences, measured or estimated by the design panel on `runs/_rap_arl_r3cblk7` and `runs/_rap_arl_n300blk7`:

- **Coverage.** At 60 stations the tree searches about 344 DDs per band where the cycle lattice of the arcs has about 370 independent directions (arcs minus stations minus satellites plus components, E - V + C; the panel's estimate; section 7 measures 362 on the carried-ambiguity graph, and AGT-5 measures it on the AR pool).
- **Churn.** DD identities kept from one epoch to the next, p50 97.7 % at 60 stations and 95.0 % at 300; fixed DDs still in the next epoch's basis, minimum 42.9 % and 82.1 % (`scripts/simnet_ar_basis_churn.py`, the panel's measurement promoted at AGT-0; re-measured on the AGT-0 references in section 7). A hold (card ARL task 2) needs a basis whose cycles outlive the epoch, or an exact integer map between two bases.
- **One more owner.** The same graph is already grown, cut and closed by four other pieces of code, each with its own union-find and tree rule:

| owner | code at `b5592b6` | what it does on the graph |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eq34 chain datum | `filter/NetworkChainDatum.cpp` 351 to 806 | a string union-find over "R<idx>" and "S<sat>" vertices, then a two-queue BFS from the elected receiver r1 (incumbent edges first, a second pass for unreached vertices) |
| Card-4 invariant carry | `filter/NetworkInvariantCarryDriver.cpp` 73 to 90, 625 to 651, 674 to 711, 809, 873 to 911; `ZComponent` in `include/filter/NetworkZStore.hpp` | an integer union-find, the L1/L2 pair-group tree completion, the tree object and its fundamental cycles, the W rows of a tree swap |

| producer AR search | `filter/NetworkProducerAr.cpp` 386 to 478 (the `ar_pool` loop head and its `tree_basis` branch), with the `DDrow` struct at 379 to 383 | the ARL Prim tree on satellite bitmasks |

## 2. The design

`filter/NetworkArcGraph` (namespace `arcgraph`): plain value types and free functions, no state, no run-context access. Every tie breaks by the caller's declared slot order, never by an identity's own `operator<`. The arc set is always an input, because the callers run at three times (pre-solve from phase-bias keys, at SB_DDBUILD from assembly columns, post-solve from live columns). Six layers:

1. **Integer ids (the Card-4 space):** `ArcKey` (moved verbatim from `NetworkZStore.hpp`); `Dsu`, which links root(a) under root(b), the rule the chain datum, the Card-4 driver and the emit re-base already share; `singleTreeOf` (none on a cycle or several fragments, as the `ZComponent` constructor throws); `fundamentalCycle`; `promoteBridgingArcs` (the pair completion); `chordRestriction` (a W row).
2. **Typed family graph:** one constellation with one band or an L1/L2 pair; `StationId` and `SatId` slots from `common/GnssIdentity.hpp`; per band a present bit and a slipped bit, a durability score and host eligibility; `arcComponents` unites the usable arcs in the caller's order.
3. **Forests, two named rules with one output type:** `growChainForest` (the eq34 two-queue BFS) and `growBaselineForest` (the ARL Prim plus three additions: incumbent links kept first, the most durable reference satellite, completion through one common satellite). A BFS from r1 gives eq34 its short chains and would give the search a star of long baselines, hence two rules.
4. **Bases:** `shortCycleBasis` closes each chord station by station in attachment order, with the partner DD, else a DD borrowed from the earlier station sharing the most satellites, else the tree path (counted, never searched). Each cycle owns a chord absent from every earlier cycle of its band, so the basis is unit-triangular and integral; under `Full` it is a Z-basis of the E - V + C cycles per band. `ParentOnly` is exactly ARL `tree_basis`.
5. **Maps:** `cycleCoordinates` (exact back-substitution), `basisChangeMap` (W: a new cycle is fresh only when it touches an invalidated arc or leaves the old span, so a slipped reference re-references rather than discards), `combineCycles`.
6. **`CycleLattice`:** fixed cycles over band-tagged arcs in reduced echelon form with unit pivots, therefore saturated (an integer cycle in the span has integer coordinates). An offer is Added, Confirmed, Contradicted or NotPrimitive; a release eliminates one arc and drops exactly one dimension when a unit row holds it (ten held DDs on a slipped reference become nine).

**Identity.** `filter/NetworkArcLife` holds an interim life stamp per band-tagged arc until card NOM step N4 puts a generation on `ArcId`: the arc ledger's admitted generation and run start (a bridged gap is a new life), whether a `LocalCarry` entry exists (an erase is a new life), `LocalCarry` `birth_ep`, `dropped_ep` and `relevel_s`, and `amb_flag_ep`. The API: `ArcLifeStamp` (those seven fields: `adm_generation`, `adm_run_start`, `has_carry`, `birth_ep`, `dropped_ep`, `relevel_s`, `flag_ep`; equal stamps mean a continuous integer), `arcLifeStamp(ctx, ArcId)` and `arcLifeChange(before, now)` (the bit mask of the fields that differ, the release attribution). See section 3 for its relation to the suspect ledger that landed at `b5592b6`.

**Callers after the card.** Chain datum: `growChainForest` per component, `recordEdge` replayed over the returned edges in attachment order (exact, because the walk reads nothing `recordEdge` writes). Card-4 driver: the integer layer on its own ids and arc order. Census and emit re-base: `Dsu` (their outputs depend on the partition only). Search: the typed graph on live columns. Task 2's hold: the lattice, with `latticeImplied` on the current short basis for fold rows.

**Increments.** AGT-0 references; AGT-1 the module and frozen oracles, no caller; AGT-2 the ARL tree through the module; AGT-3 the Card-4 driver, census and emit re-base; AGT-4 the chain datum; AGT-5 the knob; AGT-6 the observatory; AGT-7 close-out. AGT-2 to AGT-4 each pass a same-build Release A/B.

The design was written against `f518665` (card ARL section 11). Main is now `b5592b6` (ARL sections 12 and 13: the slip ladder and three slip fixes, all default off). What changes for this card:

- **One owner per concept for arc lives, without losing what the hold needs.** `filter/NetworkArSuspect` (`ctx.carry.ar_suspect`) is the search's suspect-corner mask. Its overlap with the life stamp is exact and small: of the stamp's seven fields it reads two, `LocalCarry::dropped_ep` and `relevel_s`; and two of its causes, the free-epoch latch and the RAIM T3/T4 drop or erase, are stamped at the three sites that also write `amb_flag_ep` (the free set in `NetworkAmbFloat.cpp` and the two RAIM sites; that map is written only under `network.phase_datum_held_basis`), so they are the free and RAIM sources of `flag_ep`. The arc ledger generation and run start, `has_carry` and `birth_ep` it does not read. Its record cannot be the stamp, for three reasons: it is keyed by `LosId` (station and satellite, no signal); a corner is erased once its newest stamp leaves the window; and with `suspect_epochs` at 0, the default, nothing is written. The hold (card ARL task 2) needs the opposite of each: a per-band release (a wind-up bit releases only the freed band), stamps that persist past any window (a fix enters the lattice only after a streak of identical fixes with unchanged stamps), and a stamp that is live at the defaults. The binding rule for AGT-5 and AGT-6 is therefore as follows (their gates are unchanged; their per-band bars, the freed bands at TOW 261930 and the slip-epoch eliminations, are the ones a `LosId`-keyed or windowed record would fail):
  - the life stamp is one `ArcLifeStamp` record per band-tagged `ArcId` (station, satellite, signal), owned by `filter/NetworkArcLife`;
  - it persists across epochs with no window, and it is live whatever `suspect_epochs` is;
  - it exposes `ArcLifeStamp`, `arcLifeStamp` and `arcLifeChange` as named in section 2, the API the hold's design reads;
  - one owner per concept holds at the readers: `NetworkArcLife` and `NetworkArSuspect` share one reader of the `LocalCarry` carry stamps, or `NetworkArSuspect` later derives its carry causes from `ArcLifeStamp`; neither keeps a second record of an arc's life. AGT-5 states which.
- Line numbers in section 1 are at `b5592b6`. The frozen oracle of the ARL Prim (AGT-1) copies lines 386 to 478 of `b5592b6` (the `ar_pool` loop head through the closing brace of the `tree_basis` branch) and the `DDrow` struct at 379 to 383, suspect state included. Lines 479 onward are the star path and are not copied.
