### 112.7 2026-11-18: what makes the combined mode's code column stiff (the managing session's question on section 112.6)

From the configuration of `n41_n146` and the code. The satellite code biases are estimated (`network.estimate_sat_code_bias`) under the time-linked code datum (`code_datum_time_linked`); the offset between the two code families is realized by the family difference (`code_datum_family_difference`: the sum over satellites of d_X − d_P, in place of the combined channel's sum-to-zero); a new band-1 X column is born with the first-sighting family prior (`code_family_birth_prior_sigma_m` 0.10 m: one soft row d_X − d_P = mu over the veteran satellites, `NetworkSbasis::codeFamilyBirthPriors`, folded in `CodeBiasDatum.cpp`); the band-5 X column follows through the time-linked geometry-free pin (`sat_gf_datum` broadcast_tgd, `sat_gf_datum_time_linked`); and the column walks with `estimation.process_noise.sat_code_bias_q` 1e-10 m²/s, 0.6 mm in an hour. The column is therefore tight within its first hour (formal sigma 1 to 5 cm in hour 1, about 1 cm after) and holds what its first hours and the family-difference sum give it. Its event response to a slow wander is none: the stream steps a code-bias counter on a structural re-reference or on a value jump above 0.05 m between consecutive emissions (`code_bias_disc_jump_test`, cbjump=1 in the stream's header), and E11's 15 cm over four hours is 0.3 mm per epoch.
