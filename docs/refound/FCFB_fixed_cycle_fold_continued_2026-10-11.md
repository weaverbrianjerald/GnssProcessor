## 2. 2026-10-11: FCF-1, the detached kernel and the held solve, no caller

**What landed.** No production code calls the new functions; `foldReducedDdPriors` (the Card-4 dense fold) is unchanged and serves as the unit oracle.

- **`NetworkNormalEquations::foldCycleRowsDetached`** (new translation unit `src/core/filter/NetworkNormalEquationsDetached.cpp`, 236 lines; declarations and Doxygen in `include/filter/NetworkNormalEquations.hpp`). Input: blocks of `CycleRow` (terms on local columns, the target `zhat_m`, the hold variance `pz_m2`). Every input is validated before anything is factored, and a station touched by two blocks throws. Per block and per station, only the rows that touch the station are formed: the compact `C_r'` is solved once against the cached factor, `C_r Y` enters B as triplets, `N_sr Y` the matching columns of G, `C_r N_rr^-1 u_r` the matching entries of `z_free`. `A = Pz + B` is an `Eigen::SparseMatrix`, Jacobi-equilibrated and factored by `SimplicialLDLT` with AMD ordering behind a relative-pivot screen of 1e-12. The block's increments `dN_b = G A^-1 G'` and `du_b = G A^-1 (z_free - zhat)` are kept per block and summed.
- **`NetworkNormalEquations::solveFoldBlock`**: `A^-1 r` through a block's equilibrated factor.
- **`NetworkEstimator::applyDatumPins`** (static): lines 402 to 440 of `solve()` moved verbatim; `solve()` calls it. The one change is the range bound, `N.rows()` for `numShared()`, equal at every call.
- **`NetworkEstimator::solveHeldDetached`** (const): requires a prior `solve()` with no Card-4 DD priors and no sum-to-zero gauges (else `std::logic_error`). It copies the cached reduced normals, pins the unfolded copy and measures `float_repro_rel` from the float passed in, folds the blocks, pins with the same datum and solves once without a covariance; then per block `v`, `kappa`, the block's own `chi2`, and the joint statistic and the largest shift. Empty input, or no block that folds, returns the float itself with `ok` false.
- `CMakeLists.txt`: the new TU and the test file; `tests/test_network_detached_fold.cpp` (suite `NetworkDetachedFold`, 9 cases).

**Departures from the design, declared.**

1. *The factor type is opaque.* The design names `std::shared_ptr<const SimplicialLDLT<...>>` in `DetachedFoldBlock`. It is `std::shared_ptr<const DetachedFoldFactor>`, a struct declared in the header and defined in the new TU around that same `SimplicialLDLT`, so `NetworkNormalEquations.hpp`, which every network translation unit includes through `NetworkEstimator.hpp`, does not pull in Eigen's sparse modules.
2. *The identity is joint.* The design states, per block, `kappa = A^-1 (z_free - G' x_held - zhat) = S^-1 v` and tests `v' kappa` as chi-square with K degrees of freedom. The algebra holds over all folded rows together: the blocks touch disjoint stations, but their float cycle values are correlated through the shared border, so S is the joint matrix `Pz + B + G_f' Sigma_ff G_f` over every row. The first identity test, written per block, failed by up to a factor of 100 on every system with two or more blocks; with one block it passed. Built in consequence: `kappa` is the joint vector, block by block (the posterior cycles `zhat + Pz kappa` are the dense fold's); `chi2_joint` and `dof_joint` hold `v' S^-1 v` over every folded row; `chi2[b]` holds the block's own statistic `v_b' S_bb^-1 v_b`, which equals `v_b' kappa_b` only when one block folds and otherwise comes from a held solve of that block alone (one more shared solve per folded block; `DetachedFoldBlock` keeps `dN` and `du` per block for it). FCF-2's acceptance reads `chi2[b]` or `chi2_joint`; the design's per-block `v' kappa` would not be chi-square with K degrees of freedom on a network that splits into several station components.
3. *`ok` and the reproduction bar.* `HeldSolve::ok` is "at least one block folded and `float_repro_rel` at most `NetworkEstimator::kHeldFloatReproTol` (1e-8)", a class constant, so the bar of the design's seam lives beside the quantity it judges.
4. *A zero hold variance is legal.* `pz_m2 >= 0`; zero is a hard constraint, valid only where B alone is positive definite. The fixed-cycle seam passes `(sigma lambda)^2 > 0`, which keeps `Pz + B` positive definite, so a dependent row under it is not degenerate (it is a placement error the seam must not make); the dependent-row guard is tested under a zero hold variance, where the pivot screen catches it.
5. *One shared side effect, diagnostic.* With `network.trace_section_timers` armed (it is, on `golden_sim_refound`), the held solve goes through `solveSharedConstrained`, so it is timed and counted as a solve call: under Observe the run-end line `Network (D0)   solve ... [n solves]` (`NetworkRunTimingRunEnd.cpp` 83) counts the held solves too. `scripts/nom_td_gate.py` drops every `Network (D0)` line as wall clock, so FCF-2's identity gate does not see it; the count is the honest cost of the held solve, and FCF-2's run-end row reports its own count beside it.

**The calibration, in plain words.** Under the right integers the float cycle values scatter with their float covariance, but the statistic divides by the float covariance plus the hold variance, so its mean is K - tr(S^-1 Pz), which is K only where the hold is small against the float in every direction. The gate's pair has a wide-lane sigma of 0.06 cycle and an ionosphere-free sigma of 0.04 narrow-lane cycle, about the sim's settled float. Holds of 0.01 cycle on each band, independent, amount to 0.057 narrow-lane cycle along the ionosphere-free direction: the hold is softer there than the float itself. Three consequences, recorded for FCF-2 and not acted on here (no knob exists yet):

- the unit test at 1e-6 and the block test at 1e-4 are conservative at the default hold (fewer false releases, less power), and FCF-2's bar "settled-hour chi2/K p50 at most 1" is met by construction; the expected value belongs beside it in the report;
- a held solve at 0.01 cycle tightens mainly the wide-lane direction and leaves the ionosphere-free direction near the float, which bears on D1's bar (i) (held RMS at most 0.7 times the float's); the hold sigma for the D1 arms is to be fixed in the card before they run;
- a hold of 0.001 cycle brings the mean within 5 % of K and 0.0001 cycle within 0.1 % on this pair, with the oracle still exact (the factor screen never tripped on a positive hold).

## 3. 2026-10-11: FCF-1 review fixes, and FCF-2's pre-registration amended

**Why.** A review of FCF-1 (verdict: accept after fixes) found four major defects and two minor ones: (1) the block acceptance statistic, the only same-epoch guard the design names, cannot see one wrong (1, 1) pair (the wide-lane-preserving class card ARL section 12 says the detectors miss) among the rows of a network-sized component, and the +1 L1 power gate of section 2 does not test that case; (2) the default hold of 0.01 cycle is softer than a settled float along the ionosphere-free direction, so the statistic's mean under right integers is about 0.57 K, FCF-2's "chi2/K p50 at most 1" passes by construction and D1 bar (i) fails by construction; (3) `HeldSolve::x_s` carried the held vector even when the float-reproduction check refused it; (4) with a positive hold the pivot screen on `Pz + B` cannot trip, so a repeated or contradictory dependent row folded silently; (5) the precondition guard read the estimator's request lists, which can change after the solve, not the kernel's folded state; (6) the float-reproduction residual relative to `|u|` grows with the condition number and would refuse genuine floats on weakly determined systems. Each is fixed below; the kernel still has no caller. This section supersedes section 2's departures 3 (the reproduction measure) and 4 (a dependent row under a positive hold is no longer folded).

**What changed in the code.**

- *The x_s contract (3).* `HeldSolve::x_s` is the held estimate only when `ok`, otherwise `x_float` itself, bitwise; `ok` also requires a finite held estimate; `max_shift_m` is infinite when either vector is not finite (checked explicitly: Eigen's `maxCoeff` may skip a NaN). The Doxygen of `HeldSolve` states the contract.
- *The independence screen (4).* `foldCycleRowsDetached` equilibrates and factors `B` alone, with the same AMD-ordered sparse LDLT and the same 1e-12 relative-pivot screen, before `Pz` is added. `B` is singular exactly when the rows are linearly dependent (the per-station `N_rr^-1` are positive definite), whatever the hold. A block that fails is `dependent` and `degenerate` and folds nothing (`DetachedFoldBlock::dependent`, `b_min_pivot_rel`; `HeldSolve::dependent`). The screen on `DAD` stays behind it.
- *The statistics' means (2).* `HeldSolve::chi2_expect` (per block) and `chi2_joint_expect` state K - tr(S^-1 Pz) every epoch, from `S^-1 = A^-1 - X Sigma_h X'` with `X = A^-1 G'` kept per block (`DetachedFoldBlock::X`), `Sigma_h` the held shared covariance with the pinned columns exact (each held solve now asks `solveSharedConstrained` for it), and `diag(A^-1)` by Takahashi's selected inversion on the sparse factor's own pattern (`DetachedFoldBlock::ainv_diag`), so no dense K x K inverse is formed.
- *The precondition guard (5).* `solveHeldDetached` throws when the kernel's reduction is not current (`isReduced`), or the kernel holds folded Card-4 DD priors (`numDdPriorBlocks`) or sum-to-zero gauges (new accessor `NetworkNormalEquations::numSumZeroGauges`). A request set after the solve folded nothing and no longer blocks the call.
- *The float reproduction (6).* `float_repro_rel` is the normwise backward error `|N_p x - u_p|_inf / (|N_p|_inf |x|_inf + |u_p|_inf)`, infinite for a non-finite float; the bar `kHeldFloatReproTol` stays 1e-8.
- `CMakeLists.txt`: `/bigobj` for `tests/test_network_detached_fold.cpp` (C1128 in Debug, the precedent of five other test files).

**The (1, 1) case, measured.** A (1, 1) error keeps the wide lane, so its non-centrality on a settled pair (wide lane 0.06, ionosphere-free 0.04 narrow-lane cycle) is 212 at a hold of 0.01 cycle and 528 at 0.003, against 4 207 and 11 005 for a +1 L1 cycle. Folded among K rows it adds that non-centrality to a statistic whose bar grows with K. The unit suite now folds it on two kinds of bed, noise-free so that each statistic is its non-centrality, with the kernel's stated mean beside it; powers follow from Cantelli's inequality (the statistic's variance is at most 2 E0 + 4 nc) where it bounds them, else from the normal approximation. On the independent bed each pair is observed by rows of its own, so the error's non-centrality is the pair's at every K; on the coupled bed the pairs share a 20-column border, and holding the other pairs tightens the border the pair's float error runs through.

**FCF-2's pre-registration, amended** (binding for FCF-2; card FCF sections 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 are annotated, not rewritten):
