## 107.4 2026-11-17: HKSL's height on DOY 147 traced (the author's "Trace HKSL first"): one returning satellite re-attached at ULAB00MNG

**Method.** Four diagnostic users at HKSL on DOY 147, float, one constellation each (`_rap_nw_obs{s1f,a24f}{G,E}_147_hksl`: the float users of section 107.3 with `processing.active_systems` GPS alone or Galileo alone, engine rscb6). The network's wet delay, events and postfit residuals at HKSL and the other stations in both runs; the code geometry-free signature of section 107.2 on ULAB00MNG's links.

**Results.**
- The offset is mostly in the GPS corrections. The GPS-only float user on the single network's stream sits 10 to 25 mm low from hour 11 (3D median 13 to 25 mm), on today's stream within 6 mm (3.6 to 7.9 mm). The Galileo-only users are noisier on both streams: 7 to 12 mm low late in the day on the single network's against 2 to 10 mm on today's. The GPS family is the same in both runs, so the difference reaches GPS through the satellites' corrections, which the network estimates jointly with the receivers' wet delays and clocks.

**Reading.** HKSL's loss on DOY 147 is not the per-satellite term between the modes. It is one held-first re-attachment of a returning satellite on a combined-mode receiver, ULAB00MNG, whose link then holds a pair one near-null step and about one narrow-lane cycle away from its own data. ULAB's solution absorbs the misfit in its wet delay for about ten hours; part of it reaches the corrections of the satellites ULAB shares with its neighbours; HKSL's users take that part up in hours 13 to 15 and carry it in their float ambiguities to the end of the day. It is section 100.1's hazard (a re-host takes one link's own float as the satellite's level) in its held-first form, met here because the single network's one tree took E14 back at ULAB. Why the re-expression left ULAB–E14 inconsistent with the kept holds is not traced. The code signature of section 107.2 marks ULAB–E14 in the hours it held the pair. DOY 146's afternoon losses at the Leica receivers and JPLM (1.3 to 2.4 mm) are not traced.
