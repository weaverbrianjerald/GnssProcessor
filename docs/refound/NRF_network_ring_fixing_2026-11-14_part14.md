**Reading.** On observed data one Galileo network repairs what the separate networks lost: the pilot receivers' links are held as the combined-mode receivers' are, through the whole day, and almost no satellite is left untied (bars 1 and 2). The three failures are small in size and real. GPS gives up to 2.5 points in the middle of DOY 147. The combined-mode receivers add Galileo RAIM rows, mostly at MBAR00UGA. And the root decides the E1/E5a near-null step differently on six links of DOY 146: the defect of section 103, which section 103.3 says must be closed before any ionosphere product or third-frequency hold, here on observed data. The code geometry-free residual detects that step on held links one at a time, without truth and including the steps two runs share. It is the first truth-free instrument for the near-null and a natural basis for a guard (a held link whose code residual departs by the step is released, or moved by the pair). Nothing is built without the author's decision. Bar 6 (the users) follows when their runs end.
