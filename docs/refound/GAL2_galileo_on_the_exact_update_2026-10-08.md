## 1. The question

Card GAL (2026-09-22) put Galileo E1 and E5a beside GPS on the float-vehicle posture. Card GAL section 6.1 traced the first two to one root: every satellite clock level frozen at an ignition value. The author's decision of 2026-09-22: fix that root before adoption.

Card EXL armed the fix on 2026-10-01 (card CTU's exact coupling update; on the sim, graded from 00:30:15, the datum-free level reaches 0.09 / 0.04 / 0.02 m at epochs 16 / 60 / 240 against the control's frozen 0.16 m). No Galileo arm has run on that posture: the last GPS and Galileo arms date from 2026-09-28 (card CLV2's out-of-solve variants). The question is which of card GAL's missed bars the in-solve level recursion clears, and what remains between Galileo and adoption.

Two readings are pre-registered for the second channel family (G3):

- **R-a (card CTU section 11):** the family split is a pin and datum question of the second family, not a time-update question. The free same-band C1X column absorbs the level at ignition and the exact update does not reach it. Prediction: C1X recovery still FAILS; the per-family geometry-free pin on the `sat_gf_datum` path is the next build.
- **R-b:** card CLV's failure was a property of an out-of-solve re-base that left the free column behind. Under the exact update the C1X column is an ordinary state of the same recursion and moves with the clock through the covariance. Prediction: C1X recovery PASSES or falls by an order of magnitude.

C5X is predicted to stay at 0.027 to 0.030 m under either reading (the zero inter-family assertion of card GAL section 3.2, measured independent of the level in GAL G3, CLV G4 and CLV B2).

## 5. Risks named before the runs

- **The yaw law.** Galileo runs nominal yaw steering; seven FOC satellites sit at |beta| 3.6 to 3.9 degrees over 00:00 to 06:00 on DOY 129 (below the FOC law's 4.1-degree threshold), with noon turns of E21, E27 and E31 inside the window: a transient of up to about 5 cm in those satellites' phase per turn (estimate from the wind-up and the satellite antenna offset). Owed before adoption (card OWD O7); not a gate here.
- **The rank tolerance.** `rank_gate_tol` 1e-11 was placed on GPS-only estimability; a second clock gauge and the free C1X common mode move the spectrum. A mismatch shows as S-basis completions or withheld epochs (G1, G4).
- **The consumer's bias under the hold.** The phase-clock hold leaves each constellation's common move on the clock for the consumer's inter-system bias to absorb (q 1e-12 m2/s); a Galileo-minus-GPS drift during level convergence that is not declared as a discontinuity is not absorbed. Expected at the centimetre level in hour 1, read in G2.
- **The sim cannot falsify the combined family alone.** The fixture is on the pilot family at every station with one inter-system bias (8.000 m) at every station and receiver code biases shared by code string between GPS and Galileo. G3 emulates the split by relabelling; the field (30 combined roster stations, the consumer and 10 census sites) is the real test.
- **Cost.** Card GAL measured the field loop 4.4 times the GPS-only one; on the current golden that is about 1.2 s per epoch, a 6 h field arm in 15 to 20 minutes.
