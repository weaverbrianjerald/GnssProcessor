### 2026-11-18: rule change 11, a causal group-delay latch (added to part 2's list "Rule changes the audit suggests")

11. A key, default off, that makes the RF-0c broadcast group-delay latch causal: each satellite's value from the navigation records in force at its first use, held from then on, with no look-ahead, so that a short run is a prefix of a long one. Not built without the author's word (the manager asked on 2026-11-18); if approved, it goes before S7 (b).

**Evidence** (the second session's read, card NRF section 124; the manager checked the code and the first lines of the runs). With `network.sat_gf_datum` broadcast_tgd, the setup latches each satellite's broadcast group delay once per run: the low median of its healthy navigation records whose clock epoch lies in [t0, t0 + `max_duration_s`) (`NetworkSbasis::latchBroadcastGroupDelays` in `src/core/network/solve/Sbasis.cpp`, called from `src/core/network/driver/ApplicationNetworkSetup.cpp` near line 710). Over 24 h against 5 h on DOY 146, 22 Galileo satellites latch differently by 0.07 to 0.49 m, and the first epoch's code-bias records differ by that amount (E04: −0.4188 m on the E1 codes, −0.7510 m on E5a). A 24 h configuration stopped at 45 epochs equals `d24_n146` over the first 20 epochs, data line for data line, and a 5 h configuration stopped likewise equals the 5 h run. This is card NRF section 102.1's untraced note (the first 2 h of a 24 h run differ from a 2 h run of the same configuration). A day run's latch reads navigation records up to a day ahead, which a streamed network cannot.

**Its bearing on S7 (b).** An uninterrupted run, a run that saves its state and a resumed run have three latch windows unless the latched values travel in the snapshot or the three runs share one window; S7 (b)'s pre-registration states how the latch is handled.

### 2026-11-18: design note, the native build of the phase family (after S7 (b), before S10; no code)

**What it is.** The adopted form (card NRF section 123) renames at the intake: a combined-mode receiver's Galileo phase, Doppler, signal strength and codes are read under the pilot names (L1X as L1C, L5X as L5Q), so the network sees one signal per band and the members' own arcs do not exist; the stream repeats each identity's records under the member's names. The native build keeps every arc under its own signal and still forms one family per band: one tree per band over the arcs of both signal types, one satellite phase bias per band (the identity's), and the difference between the two signal types as a modelled term behind its own key, default off. The rename stays as the adopted form until the native build passes its bars.

**What remains after S4 to S6.** S6 split the arc from the band (`chainEdgeKeys`: the arc's own key and signal beside the satellite band's key; `chainBandSignalOf` maps an arc's signal to its band through `phaseFamilyCodeOf`, `chainArcSignalsOf` is its inverse), so the election already reads every arc by its own key and every band by its identity, and on today's runs the map is the identity. Remaining:
1. The intake: a native mode of the table that keeps the members' codes (no rename), with the roster's band role mapping both signal types to the band.
2. The candidate census: a member arc's ambiguity is paired with its band's satellite phase-bias key (the identity's), so that `groupChainKeys`, which already groups the arcs by that key's signal and keeps each arc's own key, puts a combined-mode arc in its band's group with no change of its own.
3. The ring fix: its chord table, links and maps (settled, untied, certified edges) keyed by the band's signal and the family's text, which S6 left as they were; a link keeps its arcs' signals, its family is its band pair.
4. The estimator: one satellite phase-bias column per band; a member arc's observation equation (`measurement/ObservationEquation.cpp`, one equation for the network, the users and the seeds) adds the member term where it is modelled.
5. The emitter: a member's records are the identity's value plus the modelled term (today a repeat), with the same flags and hold generation.
6. The slip layer: unchanged (it already pairs a family phase with the receiver's own member code).

**How the member term enters.** Section 107.8 measured it: a per-satellite wide-lane term of the receiver make within the combined mode (Trimble 0.026 cycle rms over the satellites, steady from day to day, at most 0.07 cycle on E12; Javad near zero; the two makes uncorrelated, r −0.02), and found a term per signal type not worth building, since it would average the makes and model neither. A station parameter (the recipe of `docs/onboarding/08_adding_a_network_parameter.md`) carries one value per receiver and band, so it cannot hold a term that varies by satellite; the receiver's own phase and code biases already absorb its constant part. The term therefore enters as a satellite-side code term per (satellite, band, receiver class), the class being the make within the combined mode read from the observation file's header (107.8's classes), with zero mean over the satellites of each (band, class), so that every receiver's common part stays in its own receiver bias. It is estimated by the network for its own solution only: the stream carries one record per signal and satellite and has no per-make record, so a user of a make keeps that make's residual (at most 0.07 cycle, 107.8), as today. Its own key, default off; a first build may carry the Trimble class alone (107.8: an engine build can start with no term and add one constant per satellite for that class).

**Bars against the adopted form.** (a) Both keys off: identical in full to the adopted form's runs of this branch (the gate of an identical-output step). (b) The native mode without the term against the adopted form, on the beds m1 (the clean two-mode bed) and h40, then DOY 146 and 147 (24 h, both roots on DOY 146) with section 118's 21 users: the bars F1 to F6 of section 123 of card NRF, the two-run audit with 0 failing Galileo checks, lost satellite-samples not above the adopted form's, and the stream's identity records equal to the adopted form's to within the solve's numerical noise (the arcs differ, so the solve is not byte-identical). (c) With the term: the Trimble-class receivers' chord wide-lane fractions narrower than without it (107.8's rms 0.026 to 0.017 cycle over satellite-class-day means), no user hold wrong by the float check, and the users' millimetre cost (section 123's owed item (b)) measured against the adopted form.
