## 1. Scope and method

A line is in scope when it reports a count of fallbacks, substitutions, drops, skips or losses and does not name the entity (station, satellite, signal, constellation, link) or the cause, so that a wrong setup or a silent loss of information reads as an anonymous number. The two first-stage readings searched about 1,150 log calls for wording such as fallback, instead, skip, drop, clamp, missing and default, and read the hits most tied to the datum, signal choice, carried priors and user corrections. The skeptical re-reading checked the top items at their lines, refuted some, and added three. The writer re-read the lines of rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 at `03dfcbc`.

"Log-only" means: the change adds or reworks log text, and may add a counter or a bounded map that nothing in the solve reads. Products, traces and every number stay identical; the changed log lines are announced before the step lands (section 4).

Verdict column: CONFIRMED = re-read at the line by the skeptic or the writer; PLAUSIBLE = reported by one reader, not re-read; DEFERRED = real, but cannot occur in the adopted cold 24 h runs.
