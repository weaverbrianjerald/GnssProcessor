## 1. What this card continues, and the request it answers

Card FCF builds fix and hold in a detached form. The author asked on 2026-10-10 for "feeding the fixed integers back into the filter (fix and hold, with its slip and re-validation responses)". The card does not write the integers into the estimator: each epoch it folds them into a second solve of the shared parameters, made from a copy of the float solve's reduced normals, and only the emission reads it. The reason, in plain words (card FCF section 2): the ambiguities carry no process noise, so the float at an epoch already holds the whole history and conditioning it on the integers at that epoch gives the fixed solution; feeding the hold back into the filter would add only cross terms, and it would let the hold confirm itself (the carry, the next search, the DD trace and post-fit QC would all read values the hold produced), so a wrong integer would outlive its own release. Detached, a wrong integer reaches only the emitted product, only while it is held, and its release takes effect at once.

Cards FCF (sections 1 to 9) and FCFB (FCF-1, FCF-2, decision D1) hold the rest. This card holds step FCF-3: the hold's memory (a ledger of fixed cycles), its release responses, its re-validation, and the slip ladder that tests them, answering the second half of the request: the slip and re-validation responses.

3. *One cause table.* `ArLatticeRelease` is extended in place, as section 4 mapped: vanished, first seen, freed, stamp change (AGT-6's four), then the landed replay's culprits, the gross-LOS Decide, contradiction, re-validation, acceptance, degenerate and seam gap. One table (`arLatticeReleaseSpec`) gives each cause its name, its trace column and its run-end text; each release is one event (an arc eliminated, the rows behind a cycle dropped, or every row), counted by cause with its dimensions.
4. *The evidence.* The life readings are the search's (AGT-6, with its echo rule), so the seam compares no stamps; the landed within-epoch replay's culprits are exported from the solve glue before its drop sets leave scope (`NetworkSolveGlueResult::we_landed`; the dead by-value alias is left as it is); the gross-LOS lists (declared lines of sight, declared stations, cold re-floats) are read only when the Decide half ran before the seam (`gross_los_emit_exclude`); otherwise they hold the previous epoch's, already read through the stamp.
5. *The seam gap* releases everything after any loop epoch without a step: a withheld epoch, or an epoch whose search did not run.
6. *No PRM response rows.* The design asked for one PRM row per cause under a new kind. A new kind in the parameter registry changes the run manifest's `param_responses` record and the run-end response table of every run, armed or not, which breaks the Off identity gate (invariant 6); and the ledger is emission-side state, not a carried instance of an estimator parameter, which is what that registry declares. The cause table is the one table, and its order test (unique names, enum order, trace columns) replaces the PRM order test.
7. *The events in their own file.* Release events go to `network_ar_lattice_events.csv` (new event kinds `release_support` and `release_all`); the fold's edges (onset, offset with its cause, the late post-fit flags) go to a new `network_fixed_cycle_events.csv`. `network_events.csv` is a pre-existing trace that the Observe identity gate freezes, so nothing is added there (the event harness's own INJECT row for a planted integer apart, on its harness arms only).
8. *Localization, stated.* Over the failing units of an epoch, each band-arc found in two or more of them is eliminated (one dimension when a row holds it at +-1); each failing unit with no such arc drops the rows behind both its cycles. A passing unit whose rows those releases took is not folded that epoch (`released`).
9. *The fold's refusals.* A unit dropped by acceptance, or in a degenerate block, drops the rows behind both its cycles; a block left float after the last pass, and the epoch-wide guards (float reproduction, a non-finite estimate, the shift bound, a kernel throw), keep the rows.
10. *Admission, stated.* A fix's streak continues when its integer is the same, its last fix was the epoch before, and none of its four arcs was put in question this epoch (a new life, the replay's or the gross-LOS lines of sight, a released arc, an arc of a dropped support); otherwise it restarts at one. A fix contradicted at an epoch is not offered at that epoch. An added row folds from the next epoch (rows carry their entry epoch; a row reduced by another takes the later epoch).
11. *The re-validation statistic* is card FCFB section 3's: T = v' Sigma_pair^-1 v with the search's own pair covariance (no hold variance), chi-square with 2 degrees of freedom at `unit_alpha` 1e-6.
12. *The planted integer* is an event-harness kind, `fixed_cycle_plant` (station, satellite, phase signal, integer `delta_cycles`, TOW): at the first epoch from its TOW at which the ledger holds the arc, every row's integer moves by the shift times the row's coefficient on the arc (`arcgraph::latticeShiftArc`), so every held cycle through the arc carries the wrong integer; it acts after the contradictions and before the re-validation, and an event that never fires fails the run.

## 6. What the releases cost

- *Acceptance releases right integers.* Every acceptance release on every bed was on a right unit (no wrong unit reached a block). *(Annotation, section 8: the parenthesis is false on the P0 S8 twin, where the stale unit's block failed acceptance at TOW 267120 and acceptance released three right units and kept the stale one, whose statistic is never the block's largest.)* At 300 stations the network block fails acceptance in most settled epochs (the float's ionosphere-free covariance is 1.4 to 1.9 times optimistic, card FCFB section 4), so the worst unit's rows are dropped up to three times per epoch: 748 acceptance releases, 2 337 dimensions, and 82 833 unit-epochs left float. The fold then folds 181 units per settled epoch out of the 1 713 pairs the search fixes, and 12 213 folded runs begin and 12 182 end in 2 h. Dropping rows on an aggregate test turns a calibration failure into lost coverage.
- *The detectors' conservatism.* The float vehicle's drop of a stale gap (stamp, change bits 48) releases 7 lines of sight per 2 h at 60 stations, and the MW refloats on clean arcs at S35000SIM (card ARL section 12) 2; at 300 stations the GF trigger's clean-arc flag on S05501SIM/G32 (card FCF section 9) and one MW refloat (S16301SIM/G15) release one line of sight each.
- *Re-validation on right integers* at 300 stations: 19 units above the bar out of 160 077 (1.2e-4, the tail card FCFB section 4 measured), 38 releases.
- *An isolated failure costs up to eight rows.* In reduced echelon form the four arcs of a DD can each be a pivot, so the rows behind one cycle are up to four per band; the planted legs lost 8 rows for one wrong arc.
- *Persistence trades coverage for stability*: at `persist_ep` 1 coverage is 100 % and acceptance releases 4.7 times as many units.

## 8. 2026-10-11: FCF-3 review fixes

**Why.** A review of FCF-3 (verdict: accept after fixes) found two major defects and four minor ones.

1. *A released wrong integer could come back.* A failing cycle released by its support (the rows whose pivots it holds) left the slipped arc's old integer in the other rows that held the arc as a non-pivot term. On the reviewer's injected (7, 9) slip on the settled held arc S07000SIM/G09 at TOW 266550 (rung 3c, P0, `runs/_rap_rvf3_s79_p0`), the stale unit G09-G03/S07000SIM-S02000SIM, held (0, 83) against the true (7, 92), was folded 6 epochs, dropped by acceptance at TOW 266730, and implied again at the same stale integer at TOW 267060 and 267360 after rows on other DDs completed the span; only the float, by then moved (T 96.2 and 198.3), refused it. 3. Section 6's "no wrong unit reached a block" is false on the P0 S8 twin, and the misattribution it hides (below) was not recorded.
4. D2(b) cannot fail on its definition and was recorded as met.
5. The index lines and the Outcomes of cards FCF and FCFB were not revised.
6. An admission contradiction released rows after the epoch's units were marked, so a unit implied from those rows would still have been folded that epoch (no bed exercised it).

**What changed** (all default off). This section supersedes section 2's items 8 and 9 where they say a cycle's rows are dropped, and section 3's `latticeSupport` and `latticeDropSupport`.

1. *A verdict on a cycle releases its arcs.* A contradiction, a failed float test with no arc shared by another failure, an acceptance drop and a degenerate block each put one cycle's integer in question without saying which of its band-arcs moved. The release now eliminates every arc of the cycle (`arcgraph::latticeEliminateArc`, at most one dimension per arc a unit row holds), so the ledger keeps exactly the part of its lattice that holds none of them and no later implication depends on their old integers. `ArLatticeScope::Support` became `Cycle`; the event `release_support` became `release_cycle`, with the rows that held any of the cycle's arcs; `arcgraph::latticeSupport` and `latticeDropSupport` are removed (no caller). The new test `NetworkFixedCycleRelease.ALoneFailureReleasesItsArcsSoTheOldIntegerCannotComeBack` builds the reviewer's case (a row through the failing cycle's arc as a non-pivot term, then two fixes on DDs that miss the arc) and fails on the FCF-3 code (the stale row survives and the stale integer is implied again).
2. *Admission marks what it took.* Steps 3 and 4 of the seam are public functions called in order, `fcyc::revalidateUnits` and `fcyc::admitFixes`; the second marks released any ledger unit an admission contradiction took (test `AnAdmissionContradictionMarksTheUnitsItTookReleased`).
3. *A lapse, measured, default off.* `network.ambiguity_resolution.fixed_cycles.lapse_ep` (int, 0 off, refused below 0, named inert under source epoch): under source lattice a unit whose pair the search has not determined for that many consecutive epochs is not folded (`UnitAction::Lapsed`, `fcyc::markLapsed`); its rows stay, and the count resets at an epoch the search determines the pair. Tests `AUnitTheSearchNoLongerDeterminesLapsesAfterLapseEp`, `NetworkFixedCycles.ReleasedAndLapsedUnitsAreNotPlaced` and the loader cases.
4. *Telemetry.* The fold's run end counts the folded unit-epochs whose pair the search did not determine at that epoch (held by the ledger alone) and the lapsed units; `scripts/simnet_fixed_cycle_grade.py lattice` reports, per wrong run, the epochs the search itself determined it, the epochs folded by the ledger alone, and whether the run's end cut it off.

## 9. 2026-10-11: FCF-5, close-out

**Standing.** Closed with both decisions failed. Fix and hold, built as the design chose it (the estimator float, fixed integer cycles folded into a detached second solve that only the emission reads), does not improve the product a consumer receives on this posture:

- **D1 (card FCFB section 4).** On the golden wire the held solve moves the consumer 0.2 %: `emit_pb_hold_phase_clock` freezes the phase-visible correction within each generation, so the hold cannot reach a float consumer (M9, confirmed). With the phase-bias holds off, the held product's within-arc phase error falls only 6 to 7 % (bar 30 %) and the consumer is 17 % (60 stations) and 41 % (300 stations) worse, from held-set switching and stiffening together.
- **D2 (sections 4 to 8).** With card ARL section 13's slip fixes, no wrong integer is folded on any bed (0 of about 100 000 folded unit-epochs) and a planted integer is released at the epoch it appears; but the releases cost too much (planted collateral 7 dimensions against 2; clean-arc releases 30 and 795 per 2 h against 2 and 5), settled coverage is 87.5 % at 60 stations and 9.3 % at 300 (bar 90 %), the seam is slow (p95 38 and 850 ms), and a slip no detector sees is held for 63 epochs on the S8 twin without the slip fixes.

**Why the hold adds so little.** The float carries every ambiguity with zero process noise, so its posterior already integrates the whole arc; conditioning it on the integers changes the shared parameters only by the cross-row terms the exact coupling update drops (the critic's prediction, card FCF). The emitted phase biases are also not integer-recovering on the armed datum (several anchors, float pin targets, no receiver phase-bias states): fixing at the producer makes the product somewhat more precise, never integer for the consumer.

**What stays.** Everything is default off, unpromoted, with no golden or ledger change and the DO-NOT-BUILD record untouched (FCF-4 was never built). The kernel (`NetworkNormalEquations::foldCycleRowsDetached`, `NetworkEstimator::solveHeldDetached`), the seam `filter/NetworkFixedCycles` (Observe, source epoch or lattice) and the ledger stay as instruments: an exact held-versus-float comparison every epoch, a per-unit test of the float against its fixed integers, and the release bookkeeping by cause.

**What this points to.** The consumer gains from integers when it can fix its own, which needs an integer-recovering datum (card ARL's theory map: the eq34 chain with integer pins and receiver phase-bias states, now on the shared tree of card AGT), not a held producer solve. The producer search, the slip fixes and the lattice remain the validation layer such a datum would need. Fixtures owed if the line reopens: S1t to S7t, G1, G1n, W1, R1; causes never exercised on a bed: replay culprit, gross-LOS, seam gap, degenerate.
