## 103. 2026-11-17: the wrong X/X holds of the per-link code-error bed (a trace), and a second draw pre-registered

**Why.** Section 102.3: on `simb_mode1d` (each Galileo code of each receiver and satellite moved by its own constant draw from N(0, 0.15 m), seed 1) run `d1_off` holds 35 wrong X/X decisions and 10 358 wrong held link-epochs on 59 links, the C/Q family and GPS none; on the clean bed `simb_mode1` none. The author asked for the trace before more repair work.

**Method** (`decision_cycles.py`, `float_chords.py`, `float_vs_code.py`, `seed_scan.py` in `_irdu_grades/scripts_s20/`; grades in `_irdu_grades/grades_family_if/`). Each Galileo hold of `d1_off` is rebuilt on its fundamental cycle: the family's tree at the decision epoch (`network_chain_edges.csv`; the L1X and L5X trees coincide edge for edge at every epoch of `d1_off` and of the clean run `m1_off37`, as do the L1C and L5Q trees), coefficients alternating from +1 on the chord. The cycle's frame integers come from the truth files as the run's audit forms them (true N plus the wind-up branch at the arc's start, arcs split at gaps over 300 s in `network_ionosphere.csv`, minus the tree edges' targets); the chord's floats at the decision are the decided row's f1 and f2 (`network_ring_fix.csv`); the injected code errors are summed over the cycle. The rebuild agrees with the run's own truth label on all 591 Galileo holds of `d1_off` (320 C/Q, 271 X/X) and on all 659 of `m1_off37`. The float chords past the decision window (refused for another cause or decided) are rebuilt the same way at every tenth epoch (7 768 chord-epochs).

**Results.**
- *The error.* Every wrong decision is one wide-lane cycle: (−3, −4) cycles on (E1, E5a) 19 times and (+3, +4) 16 times. That pair moves the ionosphere-free ambiguity by 3 × 3.949 − 4 × 2.949 = 0.051 narrow-lane cycle, the E1/E5a near-null of section 91.1, so the decision's ionosphere-free fraction cannot tell it from the right pair. At the decision the chord's wide-lane float was 0.75 to 1.24 cycles off truth (|frac_wl| 0.008 to 0.249): the float sat on the wrong wide-lane integer. All 1 535 holds of the run pass by the odds gate, the wrong ones like the right (sigma_if 0.066 to 0.146 on the wrong ones). 17 of the 35 fall within the run's first hour (0.48 to 0.96 h), the rest from 2.2 to 5.4 h.
- *How the codes reach the float.* Regressed on the cycle sums of the injected E1 and E5a code errors, the float chords' wide-lane errors give −0.39 and −0.69 cycle per m (C/Q, first half hour) and −0.45 and −0.87 (X/X). The uncombined model with the geometry known and a free slant ionosphere per line of sight predicts −0.42 and −0.76 (the ionosphere taken from the two codes, the wide lane then off by (b1 + 1.793 b5) / 4.216 times (1/λ1 − 1.793/λ5)); the Melbourne-Wübbena combination would give −0.76 and −0.57. The wide lane is set by the codes through the slant ionosphere, the E5a code most: one link's 0.15 m E5a error moves its wide-lane float about 0.11 cycle, and a chord's cycle of six links sums six such errors from the chord and its tree path.
- *Why the gates pass it.* The window, persistence and odds gates test the float's formal sigma and its stability over time. A float pulled a whole cycle by constant code errors is stable, and its formal sigma does not contain them.
- *How wrong links spread.* Of the 59 links with wrong held rows, 35 were decided wrong; the others turned wrong while held: 26 flips from right to wrong (and 22 from wrong to right) at 11 epochs, every one an epoch at which a held link that was wrong at the time entered the tree (RINGFIX `to_tree`) and the chain re-hosted its satellite's edge onto it (`rehost_carry`, a known step, the pin at its held integers). The satellite's level then moved by that link's (±3, ±4), and its other held links, kept at their integers, changed their frame truth. At 2.67 h, for one, S39000SIM E26 (decided wrong at 0.68 h) entered the tree, six held links turned wrong and two wrong ones turned right. Holds that began wrong carry 7 333 of the 10 358 wrong held link-epochs, holds that turned wrong 3 025.
- *What does not differ between the families.* The draws (E5a code spread 0.149 m on C5Q and 0.156 m on C5X; implied wide-lane spread per link 0.145 and 0.154 cycle), the weights (code 0.398 m at 40 to 60° on all four Galileo codes, `network_pf_rows.csv`), the trees (54 edges each) and the cycles (mean 5.7 and 5.6 links in the first hour). On the clean bed the families' float chords have the same wide-lane error spread (0.12 and 0.11 cycle in the persistence state) and neither holds a wrong integer.
- *The draw.* The first hour's trees agree between the clean and the code-error runs on 53 or 54 of 54 edges, so each seed's errors can be laid on `d1_off`'s first-hour cycles without a run. Seed 1 puts 25 X/X chords against 14 C/Q chords above 0.6 cycle of predicted wide-lane error; over seeds 1 to 60 the counts run from 0 to 36 on X/X and from 2 to 38 on C/Q, with no preference for either family (seeds 26, 31 and 40 put 33 to 38 on C/Q against 10 to 12 on X/X; seed 2, 3 on C/Q against 32 on X/X).
- *The feedback.* In C/Q the float chords' sensitivity to their tree path's code errors falls as right holds accumulate (−0.71 cycle per m of E5a in the first half hour, −0.24 in hours 3 to 6) while their own link's stays (−0.57 to −0.80). In X/X the path's stays (−0.87 to −0.76), the own link's grows (−0.79 to −1.11) and the scatter about the fit grows from 0.24 to 0.45 cycle. Over the run the X/X float chords past the window show a wide-lane error above 0.75 cycle at 167 sampled chord-epochs, the C/Q chords at 13, against 99 and 62 predicted from the codes alone.

**Reading.** The wrong X/X integers are wide lanes decided one cycle off at E1/E5a's near-null, where only the codes set the wide lane and the decision has no test that sees a code bias. Right holds tie the satellites' and receivers' levels to many links and average a chord's tree path errors away; wrong holds, once promoted into the tree, move whole satellites. With seed 1 the X/X family drew more large errors on its early cycles, took its first wrong decisions in the first hour, and they fed themselves, while the C/Q family took none early and its right holds damped the rest. The families are equal in every input checked; whether the early difference in sensitivity (−0.87 against −0.71 cycle per m on the tree path in the first half hour) is part of the draw or of the family is what the second draw below tests. How realistic a constant 0.15 m per code and link through a day is remains open (an observed receiver's multipath varies with the geometry over an arc).

## 105. 2026-11-17: the managing session's repair tried offline: wide-lane levels from the consensus of the links (no build)

**Why** (the managing session's message of 2026-11-17, item 2): if the wrong holds are wide lanes decided one cycle off (section 103) through levels set by one link's code (section 103.1), the repair is where a level is set: a satellite's and a receiver's wide-lane level taken from the consensus of all its links' Melbourne-Wübbena means, with each link's wide lane decided against that level. Two forms are tried here on the runs' own holds, without a build: the decision-time consensus in today's frame (section 103.1: it repairs none of the wrong decisions tried, the levels being already more than half a cycle off) and the per-link form below.

**Method** (`mw_consensus.py`). Each link's Melbourne-Wübbena mean over its arc so far is read from the bed's RINEX in the station's own Galileo mode, MW = (L1 − L5) − (f1 P1 + f5 P5) / ((f1 + f5) λ_wl) in wide-lane cycles (the wind-up cancels, equal in cycles on both bands), the arc restarting after a gap over 300 s. At each Galileo hold, the family's links with at least 20 epochs in their arc give a robust two-way circular fit of their means' fractions (receiver and satellite levels, alternating circular medians); each link's wide lane is decided against it, k = round(MW − a_r − b_s), with its residual. The hold's cycle (section 103's rebuild) is then decided by its links' k: the frame's truth needs the sum of coefficient times (k − N_wl) to vanish, the integer offsets of the levels and of the tree's targets cancelling around a cycle; a cycle with a link residual over the gate is refused.

**Reading.**
- Deciding each link against a consensus level removes the tree path's inheritance but not the number of roundings: a chord is right only if every link of its cycle rounds right, and with lasting per-link errors of 0.15 cycle a cycle of six or more links fails about as often as today's cycle float does, or is refused. On the bed's error level the per-link consensus is no repair; on the clean bed it agrees with every hold it tries.
- The consensus residual detects a link whose lasting code error lies between about a quarter and a half cycle: the cycles of the wrong decisions hold a link residual over 0.25 cycle in 30 of 35 (`d1_off`) and 10 of 13 (`d31_off`), against 47 of 659 cycles (7 %) on the clean bed. Beyond half a cycle a link's error wraps and the link looks like one with another integer and a small residual: the bracket bed's outlier on the C/Q root's tree edge, S24000SIM–E02 (seed 40; a Melbourne-Wübbena error of +0.78 cycle), shows a residual of −0.03 to −0.16 at 0.5, 1 and 3 h. A veto or a vetting of hosts on the residual is therefore partial; it misses the largest errors.
- The clean bed's per-link spread (0.07 to 0.09 cycle) equals section 96's observed spread between the converged links of one satellite (0.07 to 0.08). If the simulator's own code noise is realistic, the observed receivers' lasting code errors are closer to the clean bed than to the beds of sections 102.3 and 103.1, apart from a few links (section 96's offsets of 0.3 to 0.4 cycle). This refines section 103.1's estimate (about half the bed's): the bracket beds of section 103.1, 0.075 m with 3 % of links at 0.35 m, are the nearer case.

The network receivers of DOY 146 and 147 in either Galileo mode (27 pilot, 28 combined; the held-out user excluded); each link's Melbourne-Wübbena means over arcs split at gaps over 300 s, at a loss-of-lock flag and at a jump over 0.75 cycle; at hours 3 to 21, one robust two-way circular fit over both modes' links together (each receiver its own level, the satellites' levels shared); per satellite the term, the circular mean of the combined-mode links' residuals minus that of the pilot-mode links'. The combined mode's receivers are Trimble (Alloy 11, NetR9 2) and Javad (TRE_3 Delta 8, TRE_3 4, three others), so the term is also taken per receiver family where a satellite has two or more links of each.

**Results.**
- Size: the term spreads 0.031 to 0.053 cycle over the satellites at each sampled hour of DOY 146 and 0.025 to 0.043 of DOY 147; the largest single values are 0.075 to 0.141 and 0.057 to 0.106. E12 is the largest on both days (−0.12 to −0.13 on DOY 146 for both receiver families; −0.085 for Trimble and −0.051 for Javad on DOY 147). The combined-mode links of one satellite spread 0.05 to 0.06 cycle about their mean.
- Steadiness: each satellite's term moves 0.03 to 0.13 cycle through the day, about the size of its own error; from one day to the next the per-satellite terms correlate +0.72 (Trimble) and +0.52 (Javad).
- Structure: the Trimble and Javad terms of a satellite agree in part (correlation over the satellites +0.07 to +0.67 on DOY 146, −0.05 to +0.71 on DOY 147) and differ by 0.04 to 0.07 cycle rms; some satellites carry a steady Trimble-only offset (DOY 147, day means: E16 +0.082 against −0.000, E04 +0.061 against −0.016, E19 −0.057 against +0.037, E11 −0.069 against −0.012).

**Reading.** On real receivers the per-satellite term is small: a few hundredths of a wide-lane cycle, at most about a tenth (E12), against section 88.1's up to 0.2 cycle, which read it from the two networks' separate levels and so included their drifts. It has a part common to the combined mode (E12 on both days and in both receiver families) and a part that goes with the receiver type (Trimble's own offsets on a few satellites, steady from day to day). Section 98's one term per satellite takes the common part; the receiver-type part, 0.04 to 0.07 cycle rms, is the size of the simulator's term (sd 0.05) that the single network carried without a wrong integer on the clean bed (section 106.1).
