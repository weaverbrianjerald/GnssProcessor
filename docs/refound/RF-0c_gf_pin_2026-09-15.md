## RF-0d (pre-registered): the per-signal GF pin, the S-line declaration, and the thin-satellite phase gauge

1. **Per-signal pin:** extend `sat_gf_datum` to EVERY registered code-bias column of a satellite beyond its IF pair — each additional signal's
   GF combination against the constellation's L1 reference pinned at the same `c·T_GD` (an ISC of zero where none is broadcast, counted
   separately; the small physical ISC is absorbed by the consumer's own iono), so the sparse L2X channel's split is a datum, not a prior.
   Unit test: 0 surviving directions with C1C / C1W / C2W / C2X all registered and the prior off; the L2X column's GF direction named.
2. **Declaration:** the product's S line carries `sat_gf_datum=<mode>` (a new field, never overloading `gfpin=`), and the consumer's reader
   accepts it (a run-manifest entry too).
3. **The thin-satellite phase gauge:** the 9 residual field directions are `PB + CLK` on satellites below the phase datum's station count at
   ignition — arm `phase_datum_min_stations: 3` beside 2 as a one-knob pair and read the SBC-engaged count and the hour-1 withholds; if the
   pair does not close them, the read (which satellites, how many stations, which epochs) is the finding for RF-2, not tuned further.
**Arms (6 h cold, field and sim, `broadcast_tgd`, prior off):** RF-0d = the per-signal pin + the declaration; RF-0d + `min_stations 3`.
**Bars:** the L2X arcs' within-arc rms < 10 m and the phase-bias tail share < 60 %; the field withholds in hour 1 ≤ 5 (from 29) and 0 after;
field D_phase and D_code within ± 20 % of RF-0 (GIM) from hour 2 and the consumer per-hour p50 within ± 0.5 m from hour 2; sim declared =
observed, 720 / 720.  **Frozen reading:** all bars ⇒ **RF-0d (`broadcast_tgd`) IS the baseline** — the GIM leaves the producer, the RF-1 winner is
re-run on it, and the ledger rows are written on the author's go; the L2X tail persists ⇒ its GF split is not a datum problem but a
coverage one (the read: stations tracking L2X per satellite per epoch) and the channel is dropped from the emitted set until it is; the
withholds persist with `min_stations 3` ⇒ the phase datum's ignition needs RF-2's election rule first.
