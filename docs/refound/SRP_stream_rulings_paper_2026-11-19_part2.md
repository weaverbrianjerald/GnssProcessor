##### 2.3 Readers (MAIN 5ddc422)

- `wide_lane_integer`: no reader.

##### 2.4 What releases a held user integer (CSC correction 2)

A flag falling only removes the ambiguity from the next search. Fix-and-hold writes x and P by a pseudo-update (MAIN 5ddc422 `AmbiguityResolver.cpp:3564` [P67]), which stays until `resetAmbiguity` zeroes the row and column and marks a pending reseed (`src/core/filter/StateManager.cpp:501-505` [P66]). Of the stream's contents, three trigger that reset: a phase counter step on the plain path (`src/core/realtime/RealTimeEngineSsrBreaks.cpp:303-312` [P62]), a hold-generation change (`:686-687` [P64]) and a generation-id change, which resets every ambiguity (`:189-198` [P60]). A held key (one with `hold_generation != 0`, `:677` [P63]) skips the plain path (`:301-302` [P61]). The pending reseed also makes the resolver forget the held pairs of that satellite (part reader-code-costs, `StateManager.cpp:1883-1886`, `AmbiguityResolver.cpp:244-258`).

##### 2.5 The flags on the adopted stream (measured)

From `tables/flag_events_by_day.csv` (8 files):
- No flag falls without a phase counter step in the same emission (0 in 8 of 8 files, gaps, vanishes and returns included). All six signals fall 48 to 60 times per day.
- On the five root days a held flag falls only when its satellite returns after a gap of 6 to 116 E blocks; each WTZR00DEU run adds 2 consecutive falls.
- 48 to 60 flagged keys per root day leave the stream while flagged (column vanish_flagged); each falls at its return, with a step. A user sees the key vanish before the fall.
- Every rise is between consecutive emissions and carries a step.
- `wide_lane_integer` is 1 on every P record; members repeat their identities' flags.

#### 3. The counter rules as built

##### 3.1 Writer rules (CHAIN e7bc098; CSC 3.1 re-cited)

Phase counter (P, per satellite and signal), stepping on a key with a baseline when any of these holds (`NetworkProductEmitter.cpp:938-941` [P14]; the rule block `:875-990` [W18]):
1. a constellation datum mark or a satellite mark on the phase channel (CSC 3.1 items 1 and 2);
2. a value jump on a fresh baseline, replaced by the phase-visible verdict when `network.disc_phase_visible` is armed and by the step-class verdict for classified keys (`:908-918` [P11]);
3. a release mark from the chain, only when a step-class family is set and the chain record is armed (`:920-929` [P12]; `EmitStore.cpp:472-473` [P07]);
4. unsettled steps: a held family's key at every emission where its two flags are not both up, and once at settling; with the unheld form, every signal of no held family at every emission (`:652-698` [P09, P10]); counted for the run-end line before the step (`:930-937` [P13]);
5. a continuity-guard trip (`:126-158` [P19]);
6. a member takes its identity's counter after all of the above (`:984`, `:987` [P16, P18]).

Code counter (B): forced by a constellation mark or a code-channel satellite mark; on a fresh baseline, a change of the (constellation, signal) set or of the satellite's band tuple, or an opt-in value jump; the member copy (`:787-854` [W19], member copy `:844-855` [P80]).

Clock counter (C, per satellite, only with the clock counter channel): forced by a constellation mark, a clock-channel satellite mark or an opt-in set change; no magnitude test (`:743-760` [W20]).

##### 3.2 Reader rules (MAIN 5ddc422; CSC 3.2 re-cited)

The order is generation change, status log, phase, code, clock (`src/core/realtime/RealTimeEngine.cpp:543-547` [P59]; CSC's `:543-549` and its correction's `:542-546` both name this block).
1. Generation-id change: every ambiguity reset (`RealTimeEngineSsrBreaks.cpp:189-198` [P60]).
2. Phase: the hold path claims a key with `hold_generation != 0` (`:677` [P63]); a claimed key skips the plain path (`:301-302` [P61]); otherwise translate or carry when armed, else the plain reset (`:303-312` [P62]). `noteSsrDiscontinuity` records at first sighting and reports a change in either direction (`src/core/filter/StateManager.cpp:448-459` [P65]).
3. Code: receiver code-bias re-inflation, behind `estimation.estimate_ifb` (`RealTimeEngineSsrBreaks.cpp:848-856` [P78]).
4. Clock: `reinflateOnClockDisc` (`:893` [P79]), effective only with `estimation.clock_disc_isb_reinflate_std` > 0 (CSC 3.2).

Only modelled signals are read, so a step on an untracked signal is seen at re-acquisition (CSC 3.2).

##### 3.3 The counters on the adopted stream (measured)

From `tables/flag_events_by_day.csv`:
- Held keys step at every emission while they or their partner are unflagged (100 % of such key-epochs in every file; 13 704 on DOY 146) and once at settling (164 to 170 per day); the file sums equal the run-end lines of `stdout.txt` in every file.
- With both flags up and no settling, held keys step 0.06 to 0.31 times per 1000 key-epochs.
- Clock counters step only at satellites named on an R record of the same block (31 to 60 steps per file). Code counters step at 0.70 to 0.87 % of key-epochs.
- No counter decreases (P, B, C).

#### 4. The age rules (MAIN 5ddc422; CSC 1.9)

- One rule ages every record: a record never stamped is never fresh; a record more than max_age ahead of the observation is refused (`src/core/products/SsrCorrectionStore.cpp:27-43` [U19]).
- Orbit and clock: `processing.ssr_max_age_s`, default 30 s, widened for the clock by `ssr_clock_stale_hold_factor` (default 0, off) (CSC 1.9; parse `src/core/config/GnssConfigLoad.cpp:606-607` [U20]).
- Code and phase bias: aged only under `processing.enable_ssr_bias_max_age` (default false, `GnssConfigLoad.cpp:613-614` [P76]); otherwise the last record applies indefinitely. The derived user configuration arms the gate when the key is absent (`src/core/config/GnssConfigLoadNetwork.cpp:13580-13582`, part reader-code-costs [U20, U28]); the 210 five-day users leave it unset (`tables/arming_scan_users.csv`), so their biases age at the clock limit times the hold factor (inferred from the derived configuration).
- The per-epoch coverage line uses the store default of 60 s (CSC 1.9).
- Every push of a record kind except slant ionosphere overwrites the satellite's header, so the clock's content age in two variance terms resets with each push (CSC 1.9 and C1; variance only).

#### 5. The file against the in-process store

- **In process**, the user reads the store that the network's emitter fills with every rule of section 3.1 armed.
- **From the file**, the user rebuilds an emitter from the S line (`makeReplayEmitter`, eight constructor parameters at CHAIN e7bc098 and MAIN 5ddc422 `NetworkProductIO.cpp:1132-1144` [P28]; seven at rx_sat_bias 8ca1a20 `:1118-1129` [P57]) and replays every record (rx_sat_bias 8ca1a20 `src/core/app/ApplicationConsumerReplay.cpp:519-527` [P54]). The rules armed by setters at network setup only reach no file user: the step-class test, the phase-family members, the unsettled steps and their unheld form, the continuity guard (`ApplicationNetworkSetup.cpp:1582, 1600, 1603, 1610, 1614-1616` [P29 to P33]). Release marks are not serialized (`EmitStore.cpp:472-473` [P07]). `setPhaseFamilyMembers` does not exist at MAIN 5ddc422.
- **With serialized counters** (`network.serialize_disc_counters`, default false, `GnssConfigLoadNetwork.cpp:11780-11782` [P38]; forced on by service mode, `:11928-11929` [P39]), the reader compares its recompute with the file, warns on a mismatch and adopts the file's counters (CHAIN e7bc098 and MAIN 5ddc422 `NetworkProductIO.cpp:920`, `:946-956` [P26, P27]; rx_sat_bias 8ca1a20 `:911`, `:946` [P55, P56]).
- **Without serialized counters** the reader neither compares nor adopts nor warns: a file user outside service mode keeps its own recompute, silently.
- **Measured on the adopted files** (`tables/replay_emulation_vs_stdout.csv`, `tables/replay_disagreement_by_class.csv`): on a full-file replay the recompute disagrees with the file at 959, 1153, 1088, 1181 and 1026 of 2880 epochs on DOY 143 to 147, all on the phase channel. On DOY 146, 98.2 % of the disagreeing values are unsettled steps and their member copies; the rest are step-class verdicts that suppressed a value-test step (replay-only) and steps no rule in the file explains (release marks, inferred). An emulation of the users' replay reproduces every logged epoch at 100 %.
- **Reference configurations** leave the counters out by a manifest exception (`config/golden/golden_manifest.json:200, 329, 355` [P44 to P46]); the ledger row is RECOMMEND_SCOPED (`docs/CONFIG_promotion_ledger.json:676` [P47]).
