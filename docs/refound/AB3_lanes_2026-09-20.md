## 1. The lane layer cannot return as built (2026-09-20)

A sim arm with only `network.lane_carry.enable: true` added to the working base
fails at load: "network.lane_carry.enable requires network.carry_invariant AND
network.mw_wl.ewl_enable: the lane-coordinate carry restructures the invariant
carry's pair groups and consumes the WL-2 MW/bridge statistics as its lane
measurements." The invariant carry is the retired field vehicle and is exclusive
with the float vehicle; the extra-wide lane needs L5, which is parked (AB2). Even the
retired sim golden ran with the lane layer OFF (a measured net loss at 63 and 305
stations once settled), and the standing verdict on the old vehicle was that lanes as
conditioning are net-harmful, with over-retention as the visible mechanism.

## 2. What a lane add-back means on this posture

Under the float vehicle the ambiguities are states with covariance, and on the sim
bed their double-differenced wide lane sits at its own true integer for 99.1 % of
chords ([RF-1 steps 3 to 5](RF-1_steps345_mechanism_2026-09-15.md)). A lane add-back
is then the first integer information in the network: a wide-lane integer constraint
on the float states, band pair (L1, L2), with a validated acceptance test and both
edges as events (invariant 7: a fixed lane is a stiff quantity and needs a slip
response, invariant 1). That is a build card with its own design, and it is only
worth opening if wide-lane integers exist on the FIELD under this vehicle. On the
old vehicle the field fractions were uniform, which is zero integer information.

## 3. AB3-0: the pre-registered readiness read (trace-only)

Inputs: `logs/network_dd_fractional.csv` of the baseline pair (per signal, per
station pair and satellite against the trace's reference satellite: `dd_cyc`,
`frac_cyc`, `dd_sigma_cyc`), armed on both beds.

**Measure.** Per signal and for the wide lane formed from the L1 and L2 double
differences of the same chord: the fractional-part distribution by hour, graded
reference-free (the trace's single reference satellite is in every chord; use the
method of `scripts/simnet_dd_reref.py` and `scripts/dd_frac_reref.py`). Sim: the
share of chords at their own true integer (`scripts/simnet_amb_integer.py
--ref-free`), which must reproduce 99.1 % for the wide lane. Field: truth-free, the
concentration of the fractions against a size-matched Monte-Carlo null, observing
both null traps recorded in the project instructions (a group's own circular median
shrinks the residual by about 42 % on uniform noise; a closure test needs a null
with the double-difference structure). Report also the share of wide-lane fractions
within 0.15 cycle (uniform gives 30 %) and the narrow-lane equivalent for context.

**Decision rule, frozen.** A lane build card is opened only if, in hours 3 to 6 on
the field, the wide-lane concentration exceeds its null by at least 0.10 and at least
50 % of wide-lane fractions lie within 0.15 cycle. Otherwise lanes are recorded as
not yet supported by the field wire, with the numbers, and the sequence moves to
seams and warm resume.

## 4. 2026-09-20: AB3-0 result: wide-lane integers EXIST on the field; the decision rule PASSES

Trace-only, instrument `scripts/ab3_wl_readiness.py` (fixed seed, 400-draw
size-matched uniform nulls, self-test 10 of 10), outputs
`runs/_ab3_read_20260920/{field,sim}.txt`; the headline numbers were reproduced
independently from the trace.

| hours 3 to 6 | field | sim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wide lane L1C-L2W, concentration R against its null | 0.753 against 0.471, gap **+0.283** | 0.883 against 0.455, gap +0.429 |
| wide-lane fractions within 0.15 cycle, as traced (uniform 30 %) | **57.1 %** (independent re-read 55.0 %, median fraction 0.130 cycle) | 80.6 % (80.0 %) |
| the same, reference-free (its null is 56 to 57 %, not 30 %) | 78.2 % | 91.1 % |
| chords of at least 120 consecutive epochs: median within 0.15 cycle | 69.0 % of 42 (null 29.7 +/- 6.9; re-read 62.7 % of 51) | 95.3 % of 381 |
| within-chord standard deviation of the wide lane, p50 | 0.078 cycle | 0.089 cycle |
| band level, L1C fractions within 0.15 cycle | 29.3 % (uniform) | 41.3 % |
| truth-based, wide lane at its own true integer | no truth | 99.0 % (med error 0.030 cycle) |

The frozen rule asked for a field concentration gap of at least 0.10 and at least
50 % of wide-lane fractions within 0.15 cycle in hours 3 to 6: +0.283 and 57.1 %.
PASS. The wide lane fires from hour 1 on the field (gap +0.297). The truth-free
method agrees with truth on the sim (median 0.027 against 0.030 cycle), so the
instrument is not blind.

**What this does and does not say.** Under the float vehicle the field wire has
integer structure for the first time in this project, at the wide-lane level only.
The field BANDS read uniform, and that reading carries no weight: the formal band
double-difference sigma is 0.24 to 0.56 cycle (p90 to 1.45) and on the sim 10 to
13 % of band double differences sit at the wrong integer, so a wrapped statistic
cannot see them. No lane constraint is armed on either bed, so the concentration is
not injected. Limits: the wide lane is formable on L1C-L2W only (the trace keeps L1W
on a different reference family, 38 matched rows in 720 epochs); field coverage
swings sixfold by hour; only 42 to 51 long chords exist, and 20 of 32 station pairs
have at least half of their chords within 0.15 cycle.

**Standing record this overturns in part.** The project's verdict "network
ambiguity resolution: do not build" rested on uniform field fractions on the old
vehicle ("the prerequisite is double-difference convergence, not integer
machinery"). For the wide lane that prerequisite is now met on the field; for the
bands it is not.

**What a PASS authorizes: a design decision, not a build.** A lane card on the
float states would be the first integer information in the network and touches
invariants 1 and 7 (a fixed lane is a stiff quantity: it needs a slip response, and
both edges are events). The proposed order is observatory first: AB3-1, a
measure-only wide-lane candidate census inside the producer (float value, formal
sigma from the joint block, acceptance statistic, what the fix would be), solve
byte-identical, graded against truth on the sim (wrong-fix rate) and against
stability on the field; actuation only after that. One consequence to settle in the
same design: the phase-bias hold freezes the wire at each generation's first value,
which is harmless for a float consumer and is not yet a consistent wide-lane
fraction for an integer consumer.
