## 3. The design

The rules of step TD carry over: each step is identical output, verified by a relocation verifier and a widened gate; parameter groups come first, then a generated, character-exact cut, then data tables; a body moves to the file of the concern it serves; the new structure is written down in a header a newcomer reads first.

**EP, the epoch driver.**
- **EP0.** The 22 dead names go; the configuration-derived values the lambda recomputes (about 25, among them `fc_on`, `sec_timers_on`, the SH deviation switches, `within_epoch_armed`, the phase-datum switches, the trace switches) become `NetworkKnobs` fields; the VCE hour bucket moves to the run's carry (not snapshotted).
- **EP1.** Two types: `NetworkRun` (the run's references and the TD1a groups; `NetworkRunEngines` becomes their owner, built in place, not copyable or movable) and `NetworkEpoch` (built fresh each epoch, generalizing `EpochWorkspace`, grouped by the stage that writes each member: input, clock, slips, PASS A, columns, assembly, carry, solve, post-fit hand-off, prep). The per-epoch lambda becomes `Application::networkProcessEpoch_(NetworkRun&, const SyncedEpoch&)`; seam signatures take `(NetworkRun&, NetworkEpoch&)` before the solve and `(const NetworkRun&, const NetworkEpoch&)` plus the one member they write after it, so the compiler enforces that nothing after the solve rewrites PASS A or the columns. The spine stays a plain ordered list of about 20 stage lines (four stages can end the epoch, and today's signatures are where the data flow is written down; a uniform table would hide both).
- **EP2.** The inline blocks move to their concerns, by a generator and a verifier: the anchor election to a new `filter/NetworkPhaseAnchorEpoch.cpp`, the gap-resume census to `slip/NetworkGapResumeCensus.cpp`, the slip funnel to `filter/NetworkSlipFunnel.{hpp,cpp}` (the object invariants 1 and 7 turn on, today unnamed, with its producers and readers documented), the epoch admission to one function returning Process, Skip or Stop, and the small blocks to their existing seams; every driver-side gate moves to the seam's first line.
- **EP3.** The 21 measure-only per-epoch instruments become a declared table, `kNetworkEpochObservers`, with its order constraints in its header and a test, like `kNetworkRunEndReports`.
- **EP4.** Setup becomes builders in their concerns' files (slip-QC parameters, the census arms, the carry-driver configuration, the lane run, the emit engines, the deweight gate, the estimator configuration, the run arming), taking setup from 1 055 lines to about 60.

**SG, the solve.** A linear orchestrator whose stages hand each other typed values (the plan, the gate verdict, the solution, the within-epoch outcome, the ceiling's decision), 29 parameters to about 8. New homes: `filter/NetworkCodeBiasDatum.cpp` (the W6 code-bias datum, about 400 lines, with unit-testable helpers), `filter/NetworkClockDatum.cpp`, `filter/NetworkRankGate.cpp` (with its run-end report moved beside it), `filter/NetworkRaimWithinEpoch.cpp` (T2 and the sigma0 ceiling, a distinct tier with its own counters); the other stages go to existing seams beside their run-end reports. The five exits become a declared table (`SolveSkip`: name, status columns, trace row, heartbeat state) with one skip function replacing the copies. No new section timer (the timing artifact stays comparable).

**PB, the station assembly.** PB1a: parameter groups (the epoch layout and assembly groups shared with EP1), the 100-alias preamble removed. PB1b: the produce loop cut into a declared table of about 23 per-station steps, one contribution family per file, each file also holding that family's half of the harvest; the parallel contract becomes a type (the step receives its station's assembly and state, and a const view of the rest). PB2: the duplicated idioms become single functions; the vestigial fields go. PB3: the neighbours (`networkPassASlipScan_` 1 031 lines, `networkAppendSdEwlRows_` 583).

**HV, the harvest and the run-context file.** HV0: `NetworkRunContext.cpp` split into six files by concern with unchanged signatures (a pure move). HV1: the harvest cut by carried kind into about 25 functions in nine owner files, called from a four-parameter orchestrator in today's order; the F3 block layout becomes one declared value; the receiver code-bias run counters move from carry state to the service counters. HV2: card NOM step N4's entity tables then each edit one owner file.

## 2026-10-13: EP1 specified (pre-registered before the build)

The full specification (types in code, the conversion of every call with every parameter's binding, the generator and verifier, the arms) is kept beside the tooling as `scripts/nom_ep1/README.md` when EP1a-0 lands; this section is its binding summary. Counts are measured at main `0e14eb9` (EP0) and are re-measured on the base the build starts from (the post-deletion main); the rules, checks and gates below are frozen now.

**The two objects.**
- `NetworkRun` (`include/app/NetworkRun.hpp`, new): the run's references (context, configuration, network block, logger, traces, setup, log directory) and the five TD1a groups, which it owns by value. `NetworkRunEngines` becomes the owner of the engines (estimator, invariant carry driver, carry VCE, prior-residual census, slip-QC channel, ionosphere deweight gate, the fast clock's PPP utilities), built in place right after the traces from a pure `networkEngineSpec` (the engines' constructor arguments, cut character-exact out of setup; no statement that logs moves). Neither copyable nor movable.
- `NetworkEpoch` (`include/app/NetworkEpoch.hpp`, new): built once per processed epoch after the admission, destroyed at its end. Members grouped by the stage that writes them, each group's writers listed in the header banner: `se`, `clock` (dt, pl_now_s, carry_warm), `funnel` (the slip funnel), `pass_a` (modeled, present, shared_phase_keys, n_dropped_sats), `columns`, `assembly` (asm_res, est_index_of, total_pins), `dd_build`, `ws`, `solve` (with `solve.handoff`, the post-fit hand-off), `ar_fixes`, `fixed_cycles`, `prep`, `emitted`. `x_emit()` is an accessor (no pointer into the epoch); one `emitted` value replaces four `n_emitted` comparisons. Deep const: no member holds a non-const reference, a pointer to non-const, a smart pointer or a `std::function`. Neither copyable nor movable, and it cannot bind a temporary.

**The seam rule.** A converted seam is `R networkX(NetworkRun& run, const NetworkEpoch& ep[, kept...][, outputs...])`: the epoch is const on both sides of the solve, and each group a seam writes arrives as a named non-const parameter that its spine line passes, so every epoch write is visible on the spine and checked by the compiler. After the solve the only outputs are `ep.solve.handoff` (RAIM, clock QC) and `ep.prep.sol` (the emitter); the driver's own writes there are its four stores (`ar_fixes`, `fixed_cycles`, `prep`, `emitted`). A converted definition opens with a generated preamble, one line per former parameter: its frozen declarator followed by `= <binding>;`, so a reference stays a reference and a copy stays a copy, writes marked `// written`; the body below is unchanged. The per-epoch lambda becomes `Application::networkProcessEpoch_(NetworkRun&, const SyncedEpoch&)` in `src/core/app/ApplicationNetworkEpoch.cpp`. At `0e14eb9`: 62 seams at 64 call sites convert (49 readers, 12 writers, one run-only), 11 keep their explicit form beside an epoch-form adapter (another caller exists: tests, or the solve's own call of the RHS-attribution trip); the eleven largest calls go from 225 arguments to 33. The solve's two `std::function` callbacks become direct calls in the T2 replay, with card WCF's writer-end counter kept between them. `subBegin` / `subEnd` become `networkSubBegin(run, b)` / `networkSubEnd(run, b)`.

**Gate G-R (relocation), frozen.** A canonical table (one row per seam, four rows files by worker; no row carries a binding) drives `scripts/nom_ep1_cut.py`; `scripts/nom_ep1_verify.py` checks the result independently of the generator, deriving every binding from the frozen source and from the new source separately (a root object, a member chain, one `&` or `*`) and accepting only eleven declared substitution classes (epoch member, owner move, the engine-spec cut, field move, copy source, value equal, accessor, the third-band callable, direct callback, bracket, type move). Checks: V1 bodies (every frozen line carried once, converted bodies code-identical below their preambles, every diff hunk in a declared region), V2 signatures, V3 bindings (per call site, per parameter, declarator kind included; this is the check card WCF's defect fails), V4 the driver's writes after the solve, V5 deep const, V6 the ordered list of calls, log calls, brackets and exits, V7 documentation, V8 completeness, V9 no new overload visible, V10 stage availability, V11 the writer banners, V12 the engine-spec cut, V13 parameter-name clashes. Sixteen negative controls are pre-registered, each a mutation that must fail its named check before a pass is believed (N1 is card WCF's shape: a culprit set bound by copy; N2 PASS A's slip-funnel parameter bound to the full funnel instead of its raw subset; N3 solution prep reading the float vector under the fixed-cycle emission; N7 a driver write after the solve; ...). A positive control: the verifier on the base commit alone passes. Permanent: `tests/test_network_epoch_contract.cpp` (every converted seam's parameter list pinned; a seam that starts writing the epoch must edit it) and `scripts/nom_epoch_lint.py` (V4, V10, V11 on any later tree, written into CLAUDE.md's seam contract).

**Gate G-EP (identical output), frozen.** `scripts/nom_td_gate.py --whole`, same-build Release, renamed executables, both sides on D: (`RAPID_RUNS_ROOT`). Arms: NOM2's 18 and G1/G2's `wcff`, `snapw`, `snapr`, `pdx`, `vce`, `dec`, `trc`, `shd`, minus the postures the REMOVE-key deletion retires (`cil`, `fcs2` and any smoke whose family is deleted, each named); new arms reaching what those do not: the A4 sigma0 withhold with the RHS-attribution trip (the solve's internal trip call), the rank-gate withhold, card FCF's fold emit (`x_emit()` selecting the held vector), the hand-off non-empty through RAIM and clock QC, a legacy-datum posture with an outage of the clock-datum station (PASS A's skip), T2 with a satellite and a station culprit, every measure-only instrument armed at once, and a GPS week rollover if a sim fixture can be pointed across one. Named unreached unless an arm shows a count: the solve's datum-failure and solve-failure exits and the T2 restore failure (static G-R coverage only), the streaming epoch source. Suite passes; cost: an idle-machine ABBA pair on both 2 h goldens within 1 %, taken against `e1a9ef0` so it also covers WCF, HV0 and EP0.

**Delivery.** EP1a-0, the tooling (scripts only, built now on the EP0 tree); EP1a-1, the types, the run's construction and the epoch function with every call still explicit (gated alone); EP1b, four workers on disjoint seam files (application seams; pre-solve and solve; emit and post-fit; diagnostics and observers), each regenerating the two shared generated files rather than hand-merging them, integrated solve first; EP1c, the compile-proven const tightenings (eight seams take the estimator const, the DD build's funnel const).
